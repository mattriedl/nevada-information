Visiting Las Vegas in December means cool, mostly dry days, holiday lights on every resort and one of the busiest event calendars of the year, from the National Finals Rodeo to New Year's Eve. The first half of the month belongs to rodeo fans, the middle is the quietest stretch, and the last week is the most crowded and expensive. This guide covers the weather and what to pack, the events and concerts worth planning around, which pools stay open, and the day trips that work (and the one that is closed for two weeks).

Quick answer

  • Weather: normal highs slip from about 60°F to 56°F through the month, with lows near 40°F and very little rain.
  • Biggest event: the Wrangler National Finals Rodeo, Dec. 3 to 12 at the Thomas & Mack Center, with the free Cowboy Christmas gift show alongside.
  • Quietest time: roughly Dec. 13 to 23, after the rodeo and before the holiday rush.
  • Heads-up: Valley of Fire State Park is closed to all visitors Dec. 1 to 14 for maintenance.
  • Snow day: Lee Canyon ski area on Mount Charleston opened Nov. 20, about an hour from the Strip.

Is December a good time to visit Las Vegas?

Yes, for most people December is a good month to visit Las Vegas, as long as you pick your dates with the event calendar in mind. The heat is long gone, so walking the Strip, hiking at Red Rock Canyon and spending a day outdoors are comfortable in a way they are not from June to September. The resorts are decorated for the holidays, and the shows, restaurants and attractions all run at full speed.

The catch is that December is really three different months:

  • Dec. 1 to 12: rodeo season. The National Finals Rodeo brings ten straight nights of competition and a city full of western wear. Hotels near the Strip and downtown fill up, especially on weekends, and country music takes over many of the showrooms.
  • Dec. 13 to 23: the quiet window. With the rodeo over and the holidays not yet started, this is usually the calmest and best-value part of the month, and a good time to see the Christmas lights without long lines.
  • Dec. 24 to 31: the holiday rush. Christmas week and the run-up to New Year's Eve are among the most expensive times of the year for rooms. If you are coming for the fireworks, book early; if not, avoid these dates.

The resorts, casinos and most of their restaurants stay open on Christmas Day, so a holiday trip doesn't mean missing out. Our Christmas in Las Vegas guide covers the light shows and what is open on Dec. 25, and the New Year's Eve in Las Vegas guide covers the fireworks, road closures and parties.

What is the weather like in Las Vegas in December?

Las Vegas is cool and dry in December: pleasant in the afternoon sun, chilly after dark. According to the National Weather Service in Las Vegas, the normal high at the airport falls from about 60°F at the start of December to 56°F by the end of the month, and the normal low drops from about 42°F to 39°F. Rain is uncommon, and snow on the valley floor is rare: the most ever recorded at the airport in a December was 3.6 inches, in 2008.

Two things make it feel colder than those numbers. The sun sets around 4:30 p.m., so evenings are long, and a breeze between the tall resorts can make 50 degrees feel like 40.

What to wear to Las Vegas in December

Pack for spring days and winter nights: layers you can add and remove, plus one warm jacket. Inside the casinos it is climate-controlled year-round, so you will spend a lot of time peeling off and putting on layers as you move between indoors and out.

  • A real jacket. A puffer or wool coat is not overkill for evening walks on the Strip, the outdoor ice rinks or a drive-through light show with the windows down.
  • Layers for daytime. Long-sleeved shirts, a sweater or fleece, and jeans or trousers cover most days.
  • Comfortable shoes. Distances on the Strip are longer than they look; resorts that seem next door can be a 15-minute walk apart.
  • Sunglasses, lip balm and lotion. The air is very dry and the winter sun is still bright.

Heading to the mountains? Add a hat, gloves, waterproof boots and a warm layer for each person, and keep blankets and water in the car.

Is there a rodeo in Las Vegas in December?

Yes. The Wrangler National Finals Rodeo, the season-ending championship of professional rodeo, runs for ten straight days, Thursday, Dec. 3, through Saturday, Dec. 12, at the Thomas & Mack Center on the UNLV campus, according to the Las Vegas Convention and Visitors Authority. Tickets are sold through Las Vegas Events and AXS. The official NFR ticket FAQ lists face values of $130.50 for a single balcony performance and an $80 "NFR Access Only" pass that gets you into the building without a seat; multi-day packages went on sale in August and the mini packages are sold out, so expect to use the resale market for most seats.

You don't need a ticket to join in. Cowboy Christmas, the huge western gift and gear show at the Las Vegas Convention Center, runs the same ten days and admission is free.

Las Vegas Bowl

The Las Vegas Bowl moves back to New Year's Eve this season: it kicks off at 12:45 p.m. Pacific on Thursday, Dec. 31, at Allegiant Stadium, with a team from the SEC facing a team from the Big Ten, Big 12, ACC, Oregon State or Washington State, and airs on ESPN (Las Vegas Bowl). The teams are announced after the regular season. A daytime game followed by the Strip fireworks makes a full New Year's Eve, but plan on heavy traffic around the stadium and the Strip that day.

Las Vegas concerts in December

December is one of the strongest concert months in Las Vegas, thanks to the rodeo crowds, the holidays and New Year's Eve. At Sphere, the Eagles play Dec. 4, 5, 11 and 12, dates added in July that extended the band's run to 68 shows (FOX5). The Backstreet Boys return to Sphere in December and play New Year's Eve there, with more shows on Jan. 1 and 2 (JamBase).

Rodeo week tilts the rest of the calendar toward country. Listings on concerts.vegas for the first weekend alone include Dwight Yoakam at Resorts World (Dec. 3), Dierks Bentley at Fontainebleau's BleauLive Theater and Trans-Siberian Orchestra's holiday show at Mandalay Bay's Michelob Ultra Arena (both Dec. 4), Barry Manilow at Westgate (Dec. 3 and 4) and Alice Cooper with Criss Angel at Planet Hollywood (Dec. 4). Downtown, the Smith Center's cabaret room, Myron's, has singers booked through the month as well.

What to do in Las Vegas in December

The best things to do in Las Vegas in December mix the holiday season with the outdoors, which is finally comfortable again. A few ideas beyond the casinos and shows:

  • See the lights. Glittering Lights at Las Vegas Motor Speedway, Ethel M's cactus garden in Henderson, the Magical Forest and the Bellagio Conservatory are all running. Our Christmas guide has dates and prices.
  • Walk the Strip at night. The cool evenings are ideal for the Bellagio fountains, the resort holiday displays and Sphere's exterior shows, most of which are free.
  • Go downtown. Fremont Street in Las Vegas proper stays lively all month and is easy to reach by bus or rideshare.
  • Hike while it's cool. December is prime hiking season at Red Rock Canyon, and Valley of Fire reopens Dec. 15.
  • Find snow. Ski, sled or just drive up to Mount Charleston for a winter day an hour from the desert.

Are Las Vegas pools open in December?

Some are, but most of the big pool complexes close or shrink for winter. Resorts usually keep one heated pool open for hotel guests with shorter daytime hours, and the cabanas, day clubs and wave pools take a break. Last winter, the official Las Vegas tourism site listed heated winter pools at Wynn, Bellagio, the Venetian, Mandalay Bay (Moorea Beach Club, while the wave pool rested) and Caesars Palace (Venus pool, while most of the Garden of the Gods closed November through February), with most open from about 9 or 10 a.m. to 4 p.m. Circa's Stadium Swim downtown is heated and open year-round. Pools and hours change from season to season, so check with your hotel before you pack around a pool day.

December day trips from Las Vegas

December is one of the best months for day trips from Las Vegas, but two of the classics have rules to know about this month.

Red Rock Canyon National Conservation Area

The 13-mile Scenic Drive west of the city takes about 40 minutes without stops, with trailheads and overlooks along the way. In November and December the drive is open daily from 6 a.m. to 5 p.m. (Red Rock Canyon Las Vegas), and from Oct. 1 to May 31 a timed-entry reservation is required to enter between 8 a.m. and 5 p.m. Book it on Recreation.gov, along with the per-vehicle entrance fee, and arrive within your window. Gates close at 5 p.m., soon after sunset, so start early.

Valley of Fire State Park

Nevada's oldest state park, with its red sandstone and the Fire Wave trail, is closed to all visitors, including on foot, from Dec. 1 through Dec. 14 for its annual maintenance closure and reopens Dec. 15, according to Nevada State Parks. Camping reservations aren't available during the closure. After it reopens, the park is open daily from sunrise to sunset, the visitor center from 9 a.m. to 4 p.m., and day use costs $10 per vehicle with Nevada plates or $15 for out-of-state vehicles. Bring water and a full tank; services inside the park are limited, and the nearest towns are in the Moapa Valley.

Lee Canyon and Mount Charleston

Southern Nevada's ski area opened for the season on Friday, Nov. 20, with a new chairlift serving the Wild West Bowl, reached from the main base by the Bluebird chair. Six new intermediate and advanced trails there are set to open Saturday, Dec. 12, and the new lift brings the skiable vertical drop to 1,000 feet (News 3 Las Vegas). Lift ticket prices change by date, so buy ahead on the resort's website.

Even if you don't ski, the drive up to Mount Charleston trades the desert for pine forest and, on many December days, snow. Check NVRoads for chain controls before you leave, carry chains or drive a four-wheel-drive vehicle, and don't park along the road where it is posted.

Boulder City, Hoover Dam and a warm soak

Quiet, historic Boulder City is a short drive southeast, and Hoover Dam and Lake Mead are just beyond it. Cool weather is also the season for the hike to the hot springs in the Black Canyon below the dam; our Nevada hot springs guide explains the routes and the seasonal trail closures.

Tips for a December trip to Las Vegas

  • Pick your week. Mid-December, between the rodeo and Christmas, is usually the calmest and cheapest. Rodeo weekends and Dec. 26 to 31 are the busiest.
  • Plan outdoor time for midday. With sunset around 4:30 p.m., hikes and drives work best between late morning and mid-afternoon.
  • Book timed entries and tickets early. Red Rock time slots, Sphere shows and New Year's Eve dinners sell out on peak dates.
  • Get around without a car on the Strip. The RTC bus network runs along the Strip and downtown, and walking between neighboring resorts is easy in the cool weather. Rent a car only for the day trips.
  • Check holiday hours. Most resorts never close, but some attractions, park visitor centers and off-Strip restaurants change hours or close on Dec. 24, 25 and 31.

Las Vegas in December: FAQ

Is Las Vegas worth visiting in December?

Yes. The weather is mild, the resorts are decorated, and the event calendar is packed with the National Finals Rodeo, holiday shows and New Year's Eve. For lower prices and smaller crowds, aim for roughly Dec. 13 to 23.

How cold is Las Vegas in December?

Normal highs run from about 60°F early in the month to 56°F late in the month, and normal lows from about 42°F to 39°F, according to the National Weather Service. Nights feel colder in the wind, so bring a warm jacket.

Does it snow in Las Vegas in December?

Rarely in the city itself; the December record at the airport is 3.6 inches, in 2008. Snow is common in the Spring Mountains, and Lee Canyon ski area on Mount Charleston is about an hour from the Strip.

When is the rodeo in Las Vegas in December?

The Wrangler National Finals Rodeo runs Dec. 3 to 12 at the Thomas & Mack Center on the UNLV campus. The free Cowboy Christmas gift show runs the same dates at the Las Vegas Convention Center.

Are Las Vegas pools open in December?

A few heated pools stay open, usually from about 9 or 10 a.m. to 4 p.m., but most large pool complexes, day clubs and wave pools close for the winter. Circa's Stadium Swim is heated and open year-round. Check with your hotel for its winter pool.

Is Valley of Fire open in December?

Not for the first two weeks. Valley of Fire State Park closes to all visitors Dec. 1 to 14 for annual maintenance and reopens Dec. 15. Red Rock Canyon is open all month as an alternative.

What should I wear in Las Vegas in December?

Layers: long sleeves and a sweater for the day, a warm jacket for the evening, and comfortable walking shoes. Add a hat, gloves and boots if you are heading up to Mount Charleston.
