About Nevada Information

An independent, plain-language guide to Nevada.

Nevada Information is an independent guide to the State of Nevada. It has a page for every incorporated city and census-designated town in the state, each of the 17 counties, and six regions, plus guides for people moving to or visiting Nevada.

Where the information comes from

Population, land area and ZIP code boundaries come from the U.S. Census Bureau (2010 and 2020 Censuses and the Vintage 2024 estimates). School data comes from the National Center for Education Statistics. Background text is adapted from Wikipedia under the CC BY-SA 4.0 license, and photos come from Wikimedia Commons with the photographer and license shown under each one. Weather forecasts load live from Open-Meteo.

What we are not

We are not part of, or endorsed by, the State of Nevada or any city or county government. For official business, follow the government links on each page.

Corrections

If something is out of date or wrong, tell us and we will fix it.
