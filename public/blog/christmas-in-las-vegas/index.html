Christmas in Las Vegas: Lights, Ice Skating, Events and What's Open

Glittering Lights, Ethel M's cactus garden, the Magical Forest, Strip ice skating, Cowboy Christmas and what stays open on December 25.

Christmas in Las Vegas means desert-sized light shows, a real ice rink on the Strip and a city that never closes, even on December 25. The season starts in early November, when the big drive-through and garden light displays switch on, and runs into the first days of January. This guide covers the best lights, ice skating, the major events, what is open on Christmas Day and what the weather is really like.

Quick answer

  • Biggest light show: Glittering Lights at Las Vegas Motor Speedway, about 3 miles of displays you drive through, Nov. 6 to Jan. 3.
  • Cheapest: Ethel M's Holiday Cactus Garden in Henderson, $3 per person, and the Bellagio Conservatory on the Strip, which is free.
  • Ice skating: Fontainebleau's pool-deck rink on the Strip (Nov. 19 to Jan. 17) or the Rock Rink at Downtown Summerlin.
  • Biggest crowds: National Finals Rodeo week and the free Cowboy Christmas gift show, Dec. 3 to 12.
  • Weather: normal highs fall from about 60°F to 56°F through December, with nights near 40°F.

When does Christmas season start in Las Vegas?

The Las Vegas holiday season starts in early November and runs until the first weekend of January. Ethel M's cactus garden lights switched on Nov. 5 this year and Glittering Lights opened Nov. 6. Downtown Summerlin's rink and Santa Chalet open Nov. 13, Fontainebleau's rink follows on Nov. 19, and Enchant opens at Las Vegas Ballpark on Nov. 20. Opportunity Village's Magical Forest opens Thanksgiving weekend.

December brings the busiest stretch. The National Finals Rodeo fills the city Dec. 3 to 12, and the week between Christmas and New Year's Eve is one of the most expensive times of the year for hotel rooms. If you have a choice, the first two weeks of December after the rodeo, or the days just before Christmas, are the calmest time to see the lights.

Best Christmas lights in Las Vegas

Five displays are worth planning around. Two are ticketed drive-through or walk-through events, two cost little or nothing, and one is a full holiday park with rides.

Glittering Lights at Las Vegas Motor Speedway

Nevada's best-known drive-through show winds for about three miles through the speedway grounds off Interstate 15 northeast of downtown, with more than six million lights, according to FOX5's season-opening report. It runs nightly, rain or shine, from Nov. 6 through Jan. 3. Tickets are sold per vehicle, starting at $39 per car or truck, and part of each ticket goes to Speedway Children's Charities. Some nights are free for veterans and military members, teachers and first responders; check the official Glittering Lights site for the calendar.

Go on a weeknight and arrive soon after opening. On December weekends the line of cars on Las Vegas Boulevard North can add an hour to the visit.

Ethel M Holiday Cactus Garden in Henderson

The chocolate maker's botanical cactus garden in Henderson is wrapped in more than a million lights for its 33rd season. It is open nightly from Nov. 5 to Jan. 3, usually 5 to 10 p.m., with shorter hours on Dec. 24, Dec. 31 and Jan. 1, and it is closed on Christmas Day. Admission is $3 per person (free for children under 2 and adults 65 and older), and the money goes to HELP of Southern Nevada and Three Square Food Bank, according to Ethel M. Santa visits nightly through Dec. 23.

Magical Forest at Opportunity Village

This holiday park on West Oakey Boulevard is the most family-oriented of the big displays. Besides the lights it has the Forest Express train, a 50-foot snow-covered slide, a carousel, mini golf and Santa visits. It is open Nov. 27 to 29, Dec. 3 to 24 and Dec. 26 to Jan. 1, from 5:30 to 9 p.m. Sunday through Thursday and until 9:30 p.m. on Fridays and Saturdays. Children 3 and under are free, and every ticket supports Opportunity Village's programs for people with intellectual disabilities (Opportunity Village).

Enchant at Las Vegas Ballpark

Enchant returns to Las Vegas Ballpark in Summerlin for its sixth season starting Nov. 20, with what it bills as the world's largest Christmas light maze, ice skating as an add-on and themed nights such as a dog-friendly Paws 'n' Claus evening. Tickets are timed, and add-ons such as a fast pass and skating cost extra; see Enchant Las Vegas for dates and prices.

Bellagio Conservatory and the Strip

The free option is still one of the best. The Bellagio Conservatory replaces its display five times a year, and the winter version, usually unveiled in mid-to-late November and kept until early January, fills the glass-roofed hall with a giant tree, oversized ornaments and seasonal flowers. It is open around the clock, so a late-night visit avoids most of the crowds. Other Strip resorts decorate their lobbies too; Wynn and the Venetian are known for elaborate holiday displays, and a walk between them makes an easy free evening.

Ice skating in Las Vegas

The main place to skate in Las Vegas at Christmas is the rink on Fontainebleau's third-floor Oasis Pool Deck, open Nov. 19 through Jan. 17. Entry to the deck is free; standard skate sessions start at $30 and locals' sessions at $15 on select dates (FOX5). Fire pits for groups can be reserved.

Off the Strip, the Rock Rink at Downtown Summerlin is open Nov. 13 to Jan. 18, with skate rental at $22, and it sits next to the outdoor mall's Santa Chalet and weekend holiday parade (Summerlin). Enchant also sells ice skating as an add-on. Year-round indoor rinks around the valley are another option when the outdoor rinks are busy.

Christmas events in Las Vegas

National Finals Rodeo and Cowboy Christmas

For ten days in December, Las Vegas becomes the capital of rodeo. The National Finals Rodeo runs Dec. 3 to 12 at the Thomas & Mack Center on the UNLV campus, and the Cowboy Christmas gift show fills the Las Vegas Convention Center the same days, open 9 a.m. to 4 p.m. with free admission (NFR Experience). It is a huge western-wear and gift market, and it gets crowded on weekends. Expect hotel prices to rise across the city during rodeo week.

Holiday parade and Santa

Downtown Summerlin holds a holiday parade with artificial snow and Santa every Friday and Saturday at 6 p.m. from Nov. 20 to Dec. 19, except Black Friday, and its Santa Chalet is open daily Nov. 13 to Dec. 24. Santa also visits Ethel M nightly through Dec. 23 and the Magical Forest through Christmas Eve.

Las Vegas Great Santa Run

Thousands of runners in Santa suits take part in Opportunity Village's charity race, held this year on Dec. 5 at the Magical Forest, with a 5K run and a 1-mile walk; races start at 2:30 p.m., and finishers get early entry to the Magical Forest (Opportunity Village).

What is open on Christmas Day in Las Vegas?

The casinos, most resort restaurants and the Strip itself stay open on December 25, which makes Las Vegas one of the easiest places in the country to spend Christmas Day without cooking. Resort steakhouses, buffets and cafes typically serve holiday menus that day, and the popular ones book up, so reserve a week or more ahead. Many locally owned restaurants away from the Strip close, so check before you drive.

Not everything stays open. Ethel M's cactus garden and the Magical Forest are both closed on Dec. 25, and many shops and some attractions close or shorten hours. The Bellagio Conservatory is open all day, as are Fremont Street and the Strip's resort atriums, which makes a walking tour of the decorations the most reliable Christmas Day plan.

Las Vegas weather at Christmas

Las Vegas is cool, not cold, in December. According to the National Weather Service, the normal high falls from about 60°F at the start of December to 56°F by late month, and the normal low drops from 42°F to about 39°F. Rain is uncommon and snow is rare: the most snow ever recorded at the airport in a December was 3.6 inches, in 2008.

Evenings at the outdoor light shows feel colder than the numbers suggest once the sun sets around 4:30 p.m., especially with a breeze. Bring a warm jacket, a hat for the open-air rinks and gloves for the drive-through if you plan to roll the windows down. Mount Charleston, about an hour northwest, is the place to find real snow; check road conditions with NVRoads before you go.

Tips for planning Christmas in Las Vegas

  • Watch the calendar. Rooms are usually cheapest in the week after Thanksgiving and in mid-December once the rodeo ends, and they climb sharply from Dec. 26 to New Year's Eve.
  • Buy timed tickets early. Enchant and the Magical Forest sell out on December weekends; Glittering Lights is easier on weeknights.
  • Plan transport. The light shows are spread across the valley, from Henderson to Summerlin and the speedway. A car or rideshare is the practical choice; the RTC bus network covers the Strip and downtown.
  • Combine nearby stops. Pair Ethel M with dinner in Henderson, or Enchant with Downtown Summerlin's rink and parade.
  • Day trips. The historic downtown of Boulder City makes a quiet half-day away from the Strip, and Hoover Dam is a short drive beyond it.

Christmas in Las Vegas: FAQ

Is Las Vegas worth visiting at Christmas?

Yes, if you like lights and big-city energy rather than snow. The resorts decorate heavily, the major light shows are among the largest in the West, restaurants stay open on Christmas Day, and the week after Thanksgiving can bring lower hotel rates than the rest of the season.

Where are the best Christmas lights in Las Vegas?

Glittering Lights at Las Vegas Motor Speedway is the largest drive-through show, Enchant at Las Vegas Ballpark has the biggest walk-through maze, the Magical Forest is best for young children, and Ethel M's cactus garden in Henderson is the cheapest at $3 per person.

Is Las Vegas cold in December?

Days are mild, with normal highs around 56 to 60 degrees Fahrenheit, but nights drop to about 39 to 42 degrees and feel colder outdoors after sunset. Pack a warm jacket for evening light shows and skating.

Does it snow in Las Vegas at Christmas?

Almost never on the valley floor. The December snowfall record at the airport is 3.6 inches, set in 2008. For snow, drive about an hour to Mount Charleston, and check road and chain conditions before you go.

Are restaurants open on Christmas Day in Las Vegas?

Most restaurants inside the Strip and downtown resorts are open on December 25, often with special holiday menus, and casino buffets usually stay open. Reserve well ahead, because the popular spots fill up, and call independent restaurants away from the Strip before going.

When is Cowboy Christmas in Las Vegas?

Cowboy Christmas runs Dec. 3 to 12 at the Las Vegas Convention Center, the same dates as the National Finals Rodeo. It is open daily from 9 a.m. to 4 p.m. and admission is free.
