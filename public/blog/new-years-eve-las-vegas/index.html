New Year's Eve in Las Vegas: Fireworks, Strip Closures, Parties and Tips

Free fireworks from the casino rooftops, a 21+ party on Fremont Street and big-name concerts. Here's how to plan the night, from road closures to what to wear.

New Year's Eve in Las Vegas is one of the biggest parties in the country: the Strip closes to cars, hundreds of thousands of people fill Las Vegas Boulevard, and fireworks launch from casino rooftops at midnight. Watching from the street is free. Downtown's Fremont Street party, the nightclubs and the big concerts are ticketed. This guide covers where to watch, what's on for Dec. 31, the road closures and bag rules, how to get around and what the weather is usually like.

Quick answer

  • When: Thursday, Dec. 31. The fireworks start at midnight and last about eight minutes.
  • Free option: stand on the Strip. Las Vegas Boulevard closed to all vehicles by 8 p.m. last year and reopened in the early morning.
  • Downtown: Fremont Street's 21+ Countdown Under the Canopy, with tickets reported from $35 (rising to $42 on Dec. 1 and $50 on Dec. 24), plus fireworks from the Plaza.
  • Big shows: Backstreet Boys at Sphere and Alex Warren at the Colosseum at Caesars Palace both play Dec. 31.
  • Bring: a jacket and a small bag. Glass, coolers, strollers and bags bigger than about 12 by 12 by 6 inches were banned on the Strip last year.

What happens on New Year's Eve in Las Vegas?

The main event is "America's Party," a free street celebration on the Strip that ends with a choreographed fireworks show from resort rooftops at midnight. The Las Vegas Convention and Visitors Authority (LVCVA) organizes the show with Fireworks by Grucci. For New Year's Eve last year, fireworks launched from ten properties: MGM Grand, ARIA, Planet Hollywood, Caesars Palace, Treasure Island, The Venetian, Resorts World, Fontainebleau, The STRAT and, for the first time, the off-Strip Palms. The show lasted about eight minutes and added a drone show for the first time, according to the LVCVA's Visit Las Vegas. Two radio stations, KOMP 92.3 and 97.1 The Point, played the soundtrack.

The LVCVA had not announced this year's launch sites by mid-November. The format has stayed much the same for years, so plan on a midnight show seen along most of the Strip. Sphere has also served as the city's official countdown clock, with a custom show on its exterior (LVCVA).

The crowds are big. Officials expected about 340,000 visitors in town for New Year's Eve last year, and Metro police put more than 1,000 officers on the Strip and downtown (Las Vegas Sun). Most of the Strip itself is in unincorporated Paradise, just south of the City of Las Vegas, and Metro police and the Nevada Department of Transportation handle the street and freeway-ramp closures.

Where is the best place to watch the fireworks?

The best free spot is the center of the Strip, roughly between Planet Hollywood and Caesars Palace, because you can see several launch sites at once. With launch sites spread from MGM Grand at the south end to The STRAT at the north, no single spot sees everything. Mid-Strip sees the most, and the crowd there is also the densest. Claim a place by about 10 p.m. if you want to stand near the front.

If you'd rather not stand in a crowd for hours, the other choices are:

  • The north or south ends of the Strip. Near Fontainebleau and Resorts World, or near MGM Grand, the crowd is usually thinner. You'll see the nearest shows up close and the rest in the distance.
  • A room with a Strip view. High rooms facing the Boulevard give the best view of all, but expect New Year's Eve prices and ask for a Strip-view room in writing.
  • A ticketed rooftop or restaurant. Bars and restaurants with views, including The STRAT's tower, sell New Year's Eve packages, many with open bars and set menus. Prices change every year, so book on each venue's own site.
  • Away from the Strip. Parking garages and hillsides around the valley give a distant view without the closures. Downtown, the Plaza puts on its own midnight fireworks.

Las Vegas New Year's Eve events this year

These are the big ticketed events announced for Dec. 31 as of mid-November. More resort parties and dinners are usually announced through December.

Countdown Under the Canopy on Fremont Street

Downtown's party covers the five-block Fremont Street Experience pedestrian mall under its LED canopy. It is 21 and over only, and gates open at 7 p.m. Tickets were reported to start at $35, down from $60 last year, then rise to $42 on Dec. 1 and $50 on Dec. 24. The lineup has ten acts on four stages, including Hoobastank and Alien Ant Farm on the 3rd Street stage, Petey Pablo and Bubba Sparxxx on the Main Street stage, DJs Wuki and Anabel Englund on the Casino Center stage, and cover bands Count's 77 and Alter Ego on the 1st Street stage (Vital Vegas). Buy only through the official Fremont Street Experience site, and check its list of what you can bring.

At midnight, the Plaza Hotel & Casino at the west end of Fremont Street launches downtown's only fireworks show. Last year Main Street was closed to traffic in front of the Plaza for the show.

New Year's Eve concerts

Two concerts lead the night. The Backstreet Boys added New Year's Eve to their "Into the Millennium" residency at Sphere, with shows on Dec. 31 and on Jan. 1 and 2 (FOX5). Singer Alex Warren plays the Colosseum at Caesars Palace on Dec. 30 and 31, with tickets on Ticketmaster (FOX5). Many resident shows add New Year's Eve performances, often at earlier times so the audience is out before the countdown. Check the start time on your ticket, and plan to be inside the venue before the Strip closes to cars.

Nightclub parties

Every major Strip nightclub throws a ticketed New Year's Eve party with a headline DJ. Tao Group alone runs parties at TAO, Marquee, Hakkasan and OMNIA, and says most of its New Year's Eve events require advance tickets or reservations (Tao Group). Admission costs far more than on a normal night, tables cost more again, and every club is 21 and over. Buy from the club's own site, and arrive early: you have to get through the resort's door checks as well as the club's.

Is the Strip closed on New Year's Eve?

Yes. Las Vegas Boulevard closes to all vehicle traffic on New Year's Eve, and the closure begins in the early evening. Last year, the Nevada Department of Transportation began closing Interstate 15 off-ramps to the Strip at 5:30 p.m., Metro started closing the Boulevard at 6:30 p.m., and the Strip was fully closed to vehicles by 8 p.m. (News 3). The LVCVA describes the closed stretch as running from Russell Road to Sahara Avenue, and it said roads typically reopen by about 2 a.m. Last year, police projected reopening by 4 a.m.

Last year's ramp and street closures, all from 5:30 p.m., included:

  • The I-15 off-ramps to Tropicana Avenue and to Flamingo Road, in both directions, and the northbound off-ramp to Harmon Avenue.
  • Eastbound Tropicana between I-15 and the Strip, and eastbound Flamingo between Valley View Boulevard and the Strip.
  • Hotel Rio Drive at Dean Martin Drive.

This year's closure plan is normally announced in the last days of December. Check NVRoads on the day. If you drive to a resort, park before 5 p.m. and expect the garage exits to back up after midnight. Many resorts also limit entry to registered guests and people with dinner, show or club reservations, so carry your confirmation.

What can you bring to the Strip on New Year's Eve?

Bring as little as possible. For last year's celebration, Metro police banned the following on the Strip and downtown:

  • glass and metal containers, including bottles and cans
  • coolers
  • strollers
  • bags larger than about 12 by 12 by 6 inches

The Las Vegas Sun reported the list also covered backpacks, luggage, fanny packs, camera bags and carts. Adults 21 and over can carry an open drink on the street, but not in a glass or metal container. Police also enforced curfew rules: minors under 18 needed a parent with them. Expect security checks at resort doors. If you're staying on the Strip, leave the big bag in your room and carry a small clear bag or just your pockets.

Cell service often slows in a crowd this size, so charge your phone, pick a meeting point in case your group gets split up, and save your hotel's address and phone number somewhere other than your phone.

How do you get around on New Year's Eve?

Walk, ride the bus or take the Monorail. Don't plan to drive or hail a car near the Strip after about 6 p.m. Last year the Regional Transportation Commission of Southern Nevada made bus rides free on all routes from 6 p.m. on Dec. 31 until 9 a.m. on New Year's Day. Strip routes were detoured around the closure. The Las Vegas Monorail, which stops behind the resorts on the east side of the Strip, ran nonstop from Dec. 31 into Jan. 2. Check the RTC and its rideRTC app for this year's offer and detours.

Rideshare and taxi pickups are moved to the edges of the closure. After midnight, expect long waits and surge pricing. Walking a few blocks east or west of the Strip, away from the crowd, usually gets you a car faster. If you have been drinking, don't drive; plan a bus, a rideshare or a walk instead.

What is the weather like on New Year's Eve in Las Vegas?

Expect a cool, usually dry night: by late December, the normal high is about 56°F and the normal low about 39°F, according to the National Weather Service. At midnight it is often in the 40s, and it feels colder after hours outdoors, especially with wind between the towers. Rain is uncommon but not impossible: last year the Weather Service gave a 60% chance of rain, and revelers stood through hours of it and some light flooding on the Boulevard.

Wear layers, a warm jacket and comfortable closed shoes, because you may walk several miles. A hat and gloves don't take much room. Umbrellas are hard to use in a packed crowd, so a hooded rain jacket is the better choice if showers are forecast. Check the Las Vegas forecast at weather.gov/vef a few days before.

Las Vegas hotels on New Year's Eve: what to know

Book as early as you can, because New Year's Eve is one of the most expensive nights of the year for Las Vegas hotel rooms. Rates climb through the week after Christmas. A few practical points:

  • Location matters more than usual. Once the Strip closes, you can't easily get a car to your hotel. A room on or within walking distance of the Strip lets you go back for a jacket or leave early.
  • Read the fine print. Check resort fees, cancellation deadlines and whether a "Strip view" is guaranteed.
  • Consider downtown or off-Strip. Downtown hotels near Fremont Street often cost less, and the Fremont party and Plaza fireworks are right outside. Off-Strip and Henderson resorts are quieter, with their own parties and a drive or ride to the fireworks.
  • Local residents often skip the Strip and go to a neighborhood resort's party, or watch the fireworks on TV and go to the Strip another night.

Is New Year's Eve in Las Vegas good for families?

It can be, but the Strip at midnight is not set up for young children. Strollers are banned, the crowd is tightly packed and many people are drinking. Families usually enjoy the night more by:

  • watching the fireworks from a hotel room or a ticketed restaurant with a view
  • seeing the decorations and the Bellagio Conservatory earlier in the day
  • picking a show with an early start time
  • celebrating "East Coast midnight," which is 9 p.m. in Las Vegas

Some resorts and communities around the valley have held earlier fireworks or family parties in past years; check their sites in December. Holiday light displays such as Glittering Lights and the Ethel M cactus garden stay open into early January. See our guide to Christmas in Las Vegas for dates and prices.

Tips for New Year's Eve in Las Vegas

  • Eat early. Restaurants run set menus at higher prices on Dec. 31. Reserve weeks ahead, or eat a late lunch before the closures begin.
  • Use the bathroom before midnight. Once you have a spot on the Boulevard, getting back into a resort can take a long time, and some resorts limit who can enter.
  • Tune in. If you're on the street, a phone and earbuds let you hear the fireworks soundtrack on the radio simulcast.
  • Plan the exit. After the show, hundreds of thousands of people leave at once. Waiting 30 to 60 minutes in a casino or bar before heading out is often faster than joining the rush.
  • Slow down on New Year's Day. Many visitors take a quiet drive to Red Rock Canyon, Boulder City or Hoover Dam, or a soak at one of the hot springs within reach of Las Vegas.

New Year's Eve in Las Vegas: FAQ

Is it free to watch the New Year's Eve fireworks in Las Vegas?

Yes. Standing on the Strip is free, and the rooftop fireworks can be seen along most of Las Vegas Boulevard. You pay only for ticketed events such as the Fremont Street party, nightclubs, concerts and rooftop packages.

What time do the fireworks start on the Strip?

At midnight. The show lasts about eight minutes, and last year a drone show came before it. Arrive by about 10 p.m. for a good spot in the middle of the Strip.

What time does the Strip close on New Year's Eve?

Last year I-15 off-ramps to the Strip closed at 5:30 p.m., Las Vegas Boulevard began closing at 6:30 p.m. and it was fully closed to vehicles by 8 p.m. It reopened early on New Year's Day. Check NVRoads and Metro announcements for this year's times.

How much are tickets to Fremont Street on New Year's Eve?

Countdown Under the Canopy tickets were reported to start at $35 this year, rising to $42 on Dec. 1 and $50 on Dec. 24. The event is 21 and over, and gates open at 7 p.m. Buy only from the official Fremont Street Experience site.

Can you drink on the Las Vegas Strip on New Year's Eve?

Adults 21 and over can carry an open drink on the street, but glass and metal containers and coolers were banned on the Strip and downtown last year. Use the plastic cups bars hand out.

Can kids go to the Strip on New Year's Eve?

Yes, but anyone under 18 must be with a parent because of the curfew, and strollers are not allowed. A hotel room or restaurant with a view is usually a better choice for young children.

Is it cold in Las Vegas on New Year's Eve?

Cool rather than cold. Late-December normals are a high near 56°F and a low near 39°F, so it is usually in the 40s around midnight. Bring a warm jacket and layers.
