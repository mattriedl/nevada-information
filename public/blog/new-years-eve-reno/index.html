New Year's Eve in Reno: Fireworks, Parties and Lake Tahoe Options

Free fireworks over Virginia Street, casino countdowns, early family shows at Mt. Rose and Heavenly, midnight at Stateline, and how to get home safely.

You can celebrate New Year's Eve in Reno with free midnight fireworks over the casinos on Virginia Street, plenty of no-cover casino bars, and bigger ticketed parties at the resorts. Lake Tahoe, about an hour away, adds lakeside fireworks at Stateline, an early show at Mt. Rose ski area and a gondola "ball drop" at Heavenly. This guide covers where to go, what it costs, how to get home safely and what the weather is like on Dec. 31.

Quick answer

  • Free fireworks: downtown Reno sets off fireworks at midnight over The Row casinos (Silver Legacy, Eldorado and Circus Circus) on Virginia Street, close to the Reno Arch.
  • Early fireworks for families: Mt. Rose ski area held a snowcat parade, a torchlight parade and fireworks at 5:45 p.m. last year. Heavenly's gondola ball drop and fireworks came at 9 p.m.
  • Lake Tahoe at midnight: Golden Nugget Lake Tahoe in Stateline has announced its Party on the Plaza for Dec. 31, 2026: 9 p.m. to 12:30 a.m., midnight fireworks, 21+, tickets from $125.
  • Getting home: RTC Washoe made its regular buses free from 6 p.m. to 2 a.m. last New Year's Eve. Fireworks are illegal for the public across Washoe County.
  • Weather: cold. Reno's normal Dec. 31 high is 46°F and the normal low is 25°F, and Tahoe is colder still.

What is New Year's Eve in Reno like?

New Year's Eve in Reno is a casino-district party with real winter weather. It's smaller and much easier to handle than the night on the Las Vegas Strip. Most of the action is in the downtown casinos along North Virginia Street. Here, Silver Legacy, Eldorado and Circus Circus share The Row complex, and the city's midnight fireworks go up above them. Bigger resorts outside downtown, such as the Grand Sierra Resort, Atlantis and the Peppermill, hold their own countdowns, as does the Nugget in Sparks. On the Nevada side of Lake Tahoe, the Stateline casinos and the Crystal Bay Club host the region's other big parties.

New Year's Eve 2026 falls on a Thursday. As of mid-November, only a few venues had posted this year's details. Where an event hasn't announced its plans yet, this guide gives last year's schedule as a guide and tells you where to check. Most Reno-area venues post their plans in December, and the popular dinner seatings and parties sell out in the last two weeks of the month.

Where can you see New Year's Eve fireworks in Reno?

The main free show is at midnight above The Row casinos on Virginia Street. The Reno Tahoe visitors authority describes it as a fireworks show over Virginia Street and the Reno Arch. Anyone on the street can watch for free. The blocks around the arch, between the Eldorado and Silver Legacy and the Truckee River, fill up well before midnight. Dress for standing outside in temperatures below freezing.

The show is short, so plan your evening around it rather than arriving at 11:55 p.m. A good plan is dinner downtown, then a casino bar, then step outside around 11:45 p.m. Upper-floor restaurants and some hotel rooms facing Virginia Street also have views of the show.

Elsewhere around Reno, the countdown happens indoors. Atlantis gave out party favors and a glass of champagne on the casino floor from 11:30 p.m. to midnight last year, followed by a midnight balloon drop. The Grand Sierra Resort held a casino-floor countdown with free champagne. Both were for guests 21 and over.

Reno New Year's Eve parties downtown

The Row is the center of downtown Reno's New Year's Eve, with a mix of ticketed parties and free bars. Last year the three casinos used a "Rio Carnival" theme across their bars and restaurants. Cover charges and long lines were expected at Rum Bullions Island Bar, NoVi and El Jefe's Cantina. Roxy Bar and Lounge, Bar Centro, the Topgolf Swing Suite and Cabaret were cover-free, KOLO 8 News reported. Tickets and dinner reservations are sold through Caesars' Reno events and restaurant pages.

A few other downtown spots have held their own parties. Last year, The Emerson had a midnight balloon drop and champagne toast, and Rum Sugar Lime hosted a DJ party with a champagne toast. Wild River Grille, on the Truckee River at the Riverwalk, served a prix-fixe dinner with a glass of champagne, and J Resort's all-ages LiFE iN LiTE festival filled its Glow Plaza. Expect similar events this year, but check each venue's website for 2026 details and prices.

Tips for downtown

  • Book dinner early. Restaurants run set holiday menus on Dec. 31, and prime seatings go first.
  • Bring ID. Most casino parties, bars and nightclubs are 21 and over, and you'll be carded at the door.
  • Stay close. A downtown hotel room lets you walk to the fireworks and back without driving. It's also the easiest way to grab a warmer coat halfway through the night.

New Year's Eve at Reno's resort casinos

If you'd rather skip the street crowds, the big resorts outside downtown each run a full evening. These are last year's plans. Most repeat in some form, but the details change every year.

Grand Sierra Resort

GSR's Crystal Lounge hosted live music and DJs from 6 p.m. to 2 a.m. last year. The bowling center held a no-cover DJ night, and five restaurants served New Year's dining specials. The resort's LEX nightclub held its last party before closing for a remodel. For Dec. 31, 2026, GSR has posted a casino promotion for Infinity Rewards members. It includes a noon "Noon Year's Celebration" with champagne and cupcakes (while supplies last), prize drawings through the day, and a midnight countdown with champagne on the casino floor, according to Grand Sierra Resort.

Atlantis and south Reno

Atlantis Casino Resort Spa on South Virginia Street usually serves New Year's Eve tasting menus at its restaurants. Last year it handed out party favors and champagne on the casino floor before midnight and held a balloon drop at midnight, according to Atlantis. The Peppermill, also on South Virginia Street, books up for New Year's Eve dinner, so reserve early at either one.

New Year's Eve in Sparks

Sparks is a quieter option a few miles east of downtown Reno. Last year the Nugget Casino Resort held a New Year's Eve after-party, and Sparks Water Bar threw a party with live music on the Sparks Marina. These are past events, so watch the venues' websites and the Visit Reno Tahoe New Year's page for this year's lineup. Parking is usually easier in Sparks than downtown, which is helpful if someone in your group is driving.

Lake Tahoe New Year's Eve: the Nevada side

For a mountain New Year's, Lake Tahoe's Nevada shore has midnight fireworks at Stateline, a famous concert room at Crystal Bay and an early show at Mt. Rose. Stateline sits right on the California line next to South Lake Tahoe. Expect a drive of about an hour and a quarter from Reno in good weather, and longer when the passes need chains.

Stateline casinos

Golden Nugget Lake Tahoe has already announced its New Year's Eve Party on the Plaza for Dec. 31, 2026. It runs from 9 p.m. to 12:30 a.m. on the outdoor Saltgrass Patio, with heated seating, a limited menu, a midnight champagne toast and a midnight fireworks display. Guests must be 21 or older, tickets start at $125 and they're non-refundable (Visit Lake Tahoe). Last year the resort also held an indoor Crystal Countdown party with a buffet for $149 per person, and both events were expected to sell out.

Nearby at Harrah's Lake Tahoe, the 18th-floor Friday's Station restaurant sold seats with a view of the fireworks for $20 per person last year, with a champagne toast and chocolate-dipped strawberries, the Record-Courier reported. Edgewood Tahoe hosted a formal 1920s-themed party, and Zephyr Cove ran a New Year's Eve dinner cruise on the M.S. Dixie II paddlewheeler from 6:30 to 9:30 p.m. Both need advance booking.

Heavenly Village gondola ball drop

Heavenly Village is just across the state line in South Lake Tahoe, California, and many people pair it with a night at the Stateline casinos. At 9 p.m., a lit gondola cabin comes down the mountain at the same moment the ball drops in Times Square, followed by fireworks. Last year the street party ran from late afternoon with live music, and the top of the Heavenly Village parking garage worked as a free place to watch. The event drew about 4,500 to 5,000 people in 2024, according to the Tahoe Daily Tribune. The 2026 lineup hadn't been published by mid-November.

Crystal Bay Club

On the north shore in Crystal Bay, the Crystal Bay Club's concert room usually books a big DJ or band for Dec. 31. Last year it was The Funk Hunters, for ages 21 and over, standing room only, with $50 entry and a free after-party. Check the club's calendar in December. Incline Village, a few minutes east, has quieter lake-view dinners.

Mt. Rose ski area

Mt. Rose Ski Tahoe, on the Mount Rose Highway between Reno and Lake Tahoe, puts on the best early show for families. Last year it held a snowcat parade, then a torchlight parade by the Mt. Rose Ski Team, with fireworks at 5:45 p.m. at the Main Lodge. The resort warned that parking would fill up quickly, asked people to carpool, and banned campfires in the parking lot. Because the show is over by about 6 p.m., you can be back in Reno for dinner and still see the downtown fireworks at midnight.

What is the weather like in Reno on New Year's Eve?

Reno on New Year's Eve is usually cold and dry, with a normal high of 46°F and a normal low of 25°F on Dec. 31. That's based on the 1991-2020 normals for Reno-Tahoe International Airport published by the National Weather Service in Reno. On a normal Dec. 31 there's about a 1-in-5 chance of measurable precipitation. Reno sits at about 4,500 feet, so by midnight temperatures are often in the 20s. Winters vary a lot, though: last December was Reno's warmest on record, and highs neared 50°F on the last two days of the year.

Lake Tahoe sits about 1,700 feet higher than Reno, and the ski areas are higher still, so it's colder there and snow is more likely. Wear a warm coat, a hat, gloves and boots with good grip for icy sidewalks. Check the forecast at weather.gov/rev a few days ahead.

How do you get around safely on New Year's Eve?

Plan your ride home before the night starts. Last New Year's Eve, RTC Washoe made its regular scheduled buses free from 6 p.m. until 2 a.m. on New Year's Day (RTC Washoe). Not every neighborhood has a bus route, so check routes and this year's plans at rtcwashoe.com. Rideshare prices jump right after midnight. Waiting 30 to 60 minutes in a casino before leaving is often cheaper and faster.

If you're driving to Lake Tahoe, check road conditions and chain controls for the Mount Rose Highway (SR 431), US 50 over Spooner Summit and Kingsbury Grade on NVRoads or by calling 511. Carry chains and a winter kit, fill the tank in Reno or Carson City, and don't drive mountain roads after drinking.

Leave the fireworks to the professionals. Washoe County says it's illegal to possess or use fireworks within the Truckee Meadows and North Lake Tahoe fire districts, which cover the unincorporated parts of the county. Possession can bring a fine of up to $1,000 and up to six months in jail per misdemeanor, and you can be billed for fighting any fire you start (Washoe County). The Tahoe Basin bans them too. Fireworks bought on tribal land can't legally be set off elsewhere.

What can you do in Reno on New Year's Day?

Start the year outdoors with a First Day Hike at a Nevada state park. On Jan. 1, 2026, Nevada State Parks ran guided hikes at 11 parks. Near Reno, these included a 3-mile walk from the Spooner Lake visitor center (9 to 11 a.m.), a 2.5-mile hike at Washoe Lake State Park in Washoe Valley (1 to 2:30 p.m.), a 1.2-mile walk at Mormon Station in Genoa, and a 1-mile walk at Dayton State Park. Some hikes needed registration, and all depended on the weather. The Jan. 1, 2027 schedule usually appears in December.

Other ideas for a slow New Year's Day: brunch at a casino buffet, a soak at one of the hot springs near Reno and Carson City, skiing at Mt. Rose or Diamond Peak, or the drive up to Virginia City. New Year's Eve in Reno: FAQ

Does Reno have fireworks on New Year's Eve?

Yes. Downtown Reno has a midnight fireworks show over The Row casinos on Virginia Street, near the Reno Arch, and it's free to watch from the street. Mt. Rose ski area and Heavenly Village near Lake Tahoe have held earlier shows, and Golden Nugget Lake Tahoe in Stateline has midnight fireworks for ticket holders.

What time are the New Year's Eve fireworks in Reno?

The downtown Reno show is at midnight. Around Lake Tahoe, last year's times were 5:45 p.m. at Mt. Rose, about 9 p.m. at Heavenly Village after the gondola ball drop, and midnight at Golden Nugget Lake Tahoe. Check each venue in late December for this year's times.

Is New Year's Eve in Reno family friendly?

Parts of it are. The casino parties and bars are mostly 21 and over, but children can watch the fireworks from the street downtown. The early fireworks at Mt. Rose and the 9 p.m. gondola drop at Heavenly Village are the best choices for families, since both end well before midnight.

Are the buses free in Reno on New Year's Eve?

RTC Washoe has made its regular scheduled buses free on recent New Year's Eves; last year it was from 6 p.m. until 2 a.m. on New Year's Day. Check rtcwashoe.com in December to confirm this year's hours and routes before you plan around it.

Where is the best place to spend New Year's Eve near Lake Tahoe?

It depends on your group. Stateline has midnight fireworks and casino parties, Heavenly Village has a big street party with a 9 p.m. countdown, the Crystal Bay Club has a concert, and Mt. Rose has early fireworks for families. Book rooms early, because lake hotels fill up for the holiday week.

Can I set off my own fireworks in Reno on New Year's Eve?

No. Washoe County says it's illegal to possess or use fireworks in the Truckee Meadows and North Lake Tahoe fire districts, which cover the unincorporated parts of the county, and fireworks are banned in the Tahoe Basin. Penalties can include a fine of up to $1,000 and up to six months in jail.
