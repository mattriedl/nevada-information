About Beatty

Beatty is an unincorporated town (census-designated place) in Nye County, in the Central Nevada region, with 880 residents at the 2020 Census. Because it is not incorporated, Nye County runs local services such as roads, policing and planning.

Beatty is an unincorporated town along the Amargosa River in Nye County, Nevada, United States. U.S. Route 95 runs through the town, which lies between Tonopah, about 90 miles (140 km) to the north and Las Vegas, about 120 miles (190 km) to the southeast. State Route 374 connects Beatty to Death Valley National Park, about 8 miles (13 km) to the west.

Before the arrival of non-indigenous people in the 19th century, the region was home to groups of Western Shoshone. Established in 1905, the community was named after Montillus (Montillion) Murray "Old Man" Beatty, who settled on a ranch in the Oasis Valley in 1896 and became Beatty's first postmaster. With the arrival of the Las Vegas and Tonopah Railroad in 1905, the town became a railway center for the Bullfrog Mining District, including mining towns such as nearby Rhyolite. Starting in the 1940s, Nellis Air Force Base and other federal installations contributed to the town's economy as did tourism related to Death Valley National Park and the rise of Las Vegas as an entertainment center.

Beatty, Nevada: common questions

What is the population of Beatty, Nevada?

Beatty had 880 residents at the 2020 U.S. Census.

How big is Beatty, Nevada?

Beatty covers about 17.7 square miles of land, about 50 people per square mile. By 2020 population it ranks 69th of the 133 cities and census-designated towns in Nevada.

What county is Beatty, Nevada in?

Beatty is in Nye County, in the Central Nevada region. It is an unincorporated town, so Nye County provides local government services. The county seat is Tonopah.

What is the ZIP code for Beatty, Nevada?

The ZIP code covering Beatty is 89003. Mailing addresses do not always match town boundaries, so confirm a specific address with USPS.

What area code does Beatty use?

Beatty uses area code 775, which covers all of Nevada outside Clark County.

How far is Beatty from Las Vegas?

Beatty is about 97 miles from Las Vegas in a straight line. Driving distance is longer and depends on the route.

What towns are near Beatty?

The closest is Amargosa Valley, about 34 miles away. Others nearby include Goldfield, Indian Springs, Pahrump.

What school district serves Beatty?

Public schools in Beatty are run by the Nye County School District; Nevada has one school district per county. The school a student attends depends on the home address.

What is the cost of living in Beatty, Nevada?

Nevada has no state personal income tax. Sales tax is 6.85% at minimum statewide and higher in some counties (8.375% in Clark County). Housing is the biggest cost difference between towns. See our Nevada moving guide for more on taxes and costs.

How soon do I need a Nevada license and plates after moving to Beatty?

New residents must get a Nevada driver's license and register their vehicles within 30 days of moving to the state.
