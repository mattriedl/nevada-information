About Beaverdam

Beaverdam is an unincorporated town (census-designated place) in Lincoln County, in the Eastern Nevada & Great Basin region, with 40 residents at the 2020 Census. Because it is not incorporated, Lincoln County runs local services such as roads, policing and planning.

Beaverdam is a census-designated place in Lincoln County, Nevada, United States. As of the 2020 census, Beaverdam had a population of 40.

Beaverdam, Nevada: common questions

What is the population of Beaverdam, Nevada?

Beaverdam had 40 residents at the 2020 U.S. Census.

How big is Beaverdam, Nevada?

Beaverdam covers about 0.7 square miles of land, about 58 people per square mile. By 2020 population it ranks 125th of the 133 cities and census-designated towns in Nevada.

What county is Beaverdam, Nevada in?

Beaverdam is in Lincoln County, in the Eastern Nevada & Great Basin region. It is an unincorporated town, so Lincoln County provides local government services. The county seat is Pioche.

What is the ZIP code for Beaverdam, Nevada?

The ZIP code covering Beaverdam is 89008. Mailing addresses do not always match town boundaries, so confirm a specific address with USPS.

What area code does Beaverdam use?

Beaverdam uses area code 775, which covers all of Nevada outside Clark County.

How far is Beaverdam from Las Vegas?

Beaverdam is about 110 miles from Las Vegas in a straight line. Driving distance is longer and depends on the route.

What towns are near Beaverdam?

The closest is Bennett Springs, about 2 miles away. Others nearby include Caliente, Panaca, Dry Valley.

What school district serves Beaverdam?

Public schools in Beaverdam are run by the Lincoln County School District; Nevada has one school district per county. The school a student attends depends on the home address.

What is the cost of living in Beaverdam, Nevada?

Nevada has no state personal income tax. Sales tax is 6.85% at minimum statewide and higher in some counties (8.375% in Clark County). Housing is the biggest cost difference between towns. See our Nevada moving guide for more on taxes and costs.

How soon do I need a Nevada license and plates after moving to Beaverdam?

New residents must get a Nevada driver's license and register their vehicles within 30 days of moving to the state.
