About Boulder City

Boulder City is an incorporated city in Clark County, in the Las Vegas & Southern Nevada region, with 14,885 residents at the 2020 Census.

Boulder City is a city in Clark County, Nevada, United States. It is approximately 26 miles (42 km) southeast of Las Vegas. As of the 2020 census, the population of Boulder City was 14,885. The city took its name from Boulder Canyon. Boulder City is one of only two places in Nevada that prohibits gambling, the other being the town of Panaca.

Boulder City, Nevada: common questions

What is the population of Boulder City, Nevada?

Boulder City had 14,885 residents at the 2020 U.S. Census (down from 15,023 in 2010). The Census Bureau estimated 15,029 residents in mid-2024.

How big is Boulder City, Nevada?

Boulder City covers about 208.3 square miles of land, about 71 people per square mile. By 2020 population it ranks 22nd of the 133 cities and census-designated towns in Nevada.

What county is Boulder City, Nevada in?

Boulder City is in Clark County, in the Las Vegas & Southern Nevada region. The county seat is Las Vegas.

What is the ZIP code for Boulder City, Nevada?

The ZIP code covering Boulder City is 89005. Mailing addresses do not always match town boundaries, so confirm a specific address with USPS.

What area code does Boulder City use?

Boulder City uses area codes 702 and 725, which overlay each other across Clark County, so local calls need all ten digits.

How far is Boulder City from Las Vegas?

Boulder City is about 33 miles from Las Vegas in a straight line. Driving distance is longer and depends on the route.

What towns are near Boulder City?

The closest is Nelson, about 10 miles away. Others nearby include Henderson, Whitney, Paradise.

What school district serves Boulder City?

Public schools in Boulder City are run by the Clark County School District; Nevada has one school district per county. The school a student attends depends on the home address.

What is the cost of living in Boulder City, Nevada?

Nevada has no state personal income tax. Sales tax is 6.85% at minimum statewide and higher in some counties (8.375% in Clark County). Housing is the biggest cost difference between towns. See our Nevada moving guide for more on taxes and costs.

How soon do I need a Nevada license and plates after moving to Boulder City?

New residents must get a Nevada driver's license and register their vehicles within 30 days of moving to the state.
