About Cold Springs

Cold Springs is an unincorporated town (census-designated place) in Washoe County, in the Reno-Tahoe region, with 10,153 residents at the 2020 Census. Because it is not incorporated, Washoe County runs local services such as roads, policing and planning.

Cold Springs is a census-designated place (CDP) in Washoe County, Nevada, United States. It is known as Eitse'ihpaa or Eitse'ippaa (literally "cold water" or "cold spring") in Shoshoni. It is located just off U.S. Route 395 in the northwestern part of the Reno–Sparks Metropolitan Statistical Area, adjacent to the California state line. The population was 10,153 at the 2020 census.

Cold Springs, Nevada: common questions

What is the population of Cold Springs, Nevada?

Cold Springs had 10,153 residents at the 2020 U.S. Census (up from 8,544 in 2010).

How big is Cold Springs, Nevada?

Cold Springs covers about 8.8 square miles of land, about 1,159 people per square mile. By 2020 population it ranks 24th of the 133 cities and census-designated towns in Nevada.

What county is Cold Springs, Nevada in?

Cold Springs is in Washoe County, in the Reno-Tahoe region. It is an unincorporated town, so Washoe County provides local government services. The county seat is Reno.

What is the ZIP code for Cold Springs, Nevada?

The ZIP code covering Cold Springs is 89508. Mailing addresses do not always match town boundaries, so confirm a specific address with USPS.

What area code does Cold Springs use?

Cold Springs uses area code 775, which covers all of Nevada outside Clark County.

How far is Cold Springs from Las Vegas?

Cold Springs is about 351 miles from Las Vegas in a straight line. Driving distance is longer and depends on the route.

What towns are near Cold Springs?

The closest is Lemmon Valley, about 8 miles away. Others nearby include Golden Valley, Verdi, Reno.

What school district serves Cold Springs?

Public schools in Cold Springs are run by the Washoe County School District; Nevada has one school district per county. The school a student attends depends on the home address.

What is the cost of living in Cold Springs, Nevada?

Nevada has no state personal income tax. Sales tax is 6.85% at minimum statewide and higher in some counties (8.375% in Clark County). Housing is the biggest cost difference between towns. See our Nevada moving guide for more on taxes and costs.

How soon do I need a Nevada license and plates after moving to Cold Springs?

New residents must get a Nevada driver's license and register their vehicles within 30 days of moving to the state.
