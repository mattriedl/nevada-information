About Ely

Ely is an incorporated city in White Pine County, in the Eastern Nevada & Great Basin region, with 3,924 residents at the 2020 Census.

Ely is the only city in and the county seat of White Pine County, Nevada, United States. Ely was founded as a stagecoach station along the Pony Express and Central Overland Route. In 1906 copper was discovered. Ely's mining boom came later than the other towns along US 50. The railroads connecting the transcontinental railroad to the mines in Austin, Nevada and Eureka, Nevada have long been removed, but the railroad to Ely is preserved as a heritage railway by the Nevada Northern Railway and known as the Ghost Train of Old Ely. As of the 2020 census, the population was 3,924.

Ely, Nevada: common questions

What is the population of Ely, Nevada?

Ely had 3,924 residents at the 2020 U.S. Census (down from 4,255 in 2010). The Census Bureau estimated 3,892 residents in mid-2024.

How big is Ely, Nevada?

Ely covers about 7.6 square miles of land, about 515 people per square mile. By 2020 population it ranks 37th of the 133 cities and census-designated towns in Nevada.

What county is Ely, Nevada in?

Ely is in White Pine County, in the Eastern Nevada & Great Basin region. Ely is the county seat.

What is the ZIP code for Ely, Nevada?

The ZIP code covering Ely is 89301. Mailing addresses do not always match town boundaries, so confirm a specific address with USPS.

What area code does Ely use?

Ely uses area code 775, which covers all of Nevada outside Clark County.

How far is Ely from Las Vegas?

Ely is about 211 miles from Las Vegas in a straight line. Driving distance is longer and depends on the route.

What towns are near Ely?

The closest is Ruth, about 6 miles away. Others nearby include McGill, Preston, Lund.

What school district serves Ely?

Public schools in Ely are run by the White Pine County School District; Nevada has one school district per county. The school a student attends depends on the home address.

What is the cost of living in Ely, Nevada?

Nevada has no state personal income tax. Sales tax is 6.85% at minimum statewide and higher in some counties (8.375% in Clark County). Housing is the biggest cost difference between towns. See our Nevada moving guide for more on taxes and costs.

How soon do I need a Nevada license and plates after moving to Ely?

New residents must get a Nevada driver's license and register their vehicles within 30 days of moving to the state.
