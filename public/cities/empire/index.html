About Empire

Empire is an unincorporated town (census-designated place) in Washoe County, in the Reno-Tahoe region, with 47 residents at the 2020 Census. Because it is not incorporated, Washoe County runs local services such as roads, policing and planning.

Empire is an unincorporated community and census-designated place (CDP) in Washoe County, Nevada, United States, with a population estimated at 65 (2021). Prior to the 2010 census it was part of the Gerlach–Empire census-designated place, it is now part of the Reno-Sparks metropolitan area. The nearest town, Nixon, is 60 miles (97 km) to the south on a reservation owned by the Pyramid Lake Paiute Tribe.

For 63 years, from 1948 to 2011, Empire was a company town of the US Gypsum Corporation, a manufacturer of gypsum-based construction sheetrock, and once had a population of more than 750 people. US Gypsum closed the mine and the town in 2011; the mine and town were bought in 2016 by the Empire Mining Company (EMC), a manufacturer of gypsum based agricultural and construction additives. In 2016, the town again became a company town under the new auspices of EMC. Since that date, both the town and the mine have been partially reopened by the new owners.

Empire, Nevada: common questions

What is the population of Empire, Nevada?

Empire had 47 residents at the 2020 U.S. Census.

How big is Empire, Nevada?

Empire covers about 5.1 square miles of land, about 9 people per square mile. By 2020 population it ranks 123rd of the 133 cities and census-designated towns in Nevada.

What county is Empire, Nevada in?

Empire is in Washoe County, in the Reno-Tahoe region. It is an unincorporated town, so Washoe County provides local government services. The county seat is Reno.

What is the ZIP code for Empire, Nevada?

The ZIP code covering Empire is 89405. Mailing addresses do not always match town boundaries, so confirm a specific address with USPS.

What area code does Empire use?

Empire uses area code 775, which covers all of Nevada outside Clark County.

How far is Empire from Las Vegas?

Empire is about 372 miles from Las Vegas in a straight line. Driving distance is longer and depends on the route.

What towns are near Empire?

The closest is Gerlach, about 5 miles away. Others nearby include Sutcliffe, Nixon, Lovelock.

What school district serves Empire?

Public schools in Empire are run by the Washoe County School District; Nevada has one school district per county. The school a student attends depends on the home address.

What is the cost of living in Empire, Nevada?

Nevada has no state personal income tax. Sales tax is 6.85% at minimum statewide and higher in some counties (8.375% in Clark County). Housing is the biggest cost difference between towns. See our Nevada moving guide for more on taxes and costs.

How soon do I need a Nevada license and plates after moving to Empire?

New residents must get a Nevada driver's license and register their vehicles within 30 days of moving to the state.
