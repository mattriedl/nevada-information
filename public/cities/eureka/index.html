About Eureka

Eureka is an unincorporated town (census-designated place) in Eureka County, in the Northeastern Nevada region, with 414 residents at the 2020 Census. Because it is not incorporated, Eureka County runs local services such as roads, policing and planning.

Eureka is a census-designated place in and the county seat of Eureka County, Nevada, United States. With a population of 414 as of the 2020 United States census, it is the second-largest community in Eureka County. Attractions include the Eureka Opera House (built in 1880 and restored in 1993), Raine’s Market and Wildlife Museum (built 1887), the Jackson House Hotel (built 1877), and the Eureka Sentinel Museum (housed in the 1879 Eureka Sentinel Newspaper Building).

Eureka is part of the Elko Micropolitan Statistical Area.

Eureka, Nevada: common questions

What is the population of Eureka, Nevada?

Eureka had 414 residents at the 2020 U.S. Census.

How big is Eureka, Nevada?

Eureka covers about 1.3 square miles of land, about 320 people per square mile. By 2020 population it ranks 83rd of the 133 cities and census-designated towns in Nevada.

What county is Eureka, Nevada in?

Eureka is in Eureka County, in the Northeastern Nevada region. It is an unincorporated town, so Eureka County provides local government services. Eureka is the county seat.

What is the ZIP code for Eureka, Nevada?

The ZIP code covering Eureka is 89316. Mailing addresses do not always match town boundaries, so confirm a specific address with USPS.

What area code does Eureka use?

Eureka uses area code 775, which covers all of Nevada outside Clark County.

How far is Eureka from Las Vegas?

Eureka is about 230 miles from Las Vegas in a straight line. Driving distance is longer and depends on the route.

What towns are near Eureka?

The closest is Ruth, about 55 miles away. Others nearby include Austin, Ely, Kingston.

What school district serves Eureka?

Public schools in Eureka are run by the Eureka County School District; Nevada has one school district per county. The school a student attends depends on the home address.

What is the cost of living in Eureka, Nevada?

Nevada has no state personal income tax. Sales tax is 6.85% at minimum statewide and higher in some counties (8.375% in Clark County). Housing is the biggest cost difference between towns. See our Nevada moving guide for more on taxes and costs.

How soon do I need a Nevada license and plates after moving to Eureka?

New residents must get a Nevada driver's license and register their vehicles within 30 days of moving to the state.
