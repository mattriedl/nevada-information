About Fallon

Fallon is an incorporated city in Churchill County, in the Central Nevada region, with 9,327 residents at the 2020 Census.

Fallon is a city in Churchill County in the U.S. state of Nevada. The population was 9,327 at the time of the 2020 census. Fallon is the county seat of Churchill County and is located in the Lahontan Valley. Fallon is known for being home to Naval Air Station Fallon, located southeast of the city.

Fallon, Nevada: common questions

What is the population of Fallon, Nevada?

Fallon had 9,327 residents at the 2020 U.S. Census (up from 8,606 in 2010). The Census Bureau estimated 9,647 residents in mid-2024.

How big is Fallon, Nevada?

Fallon covers about 3.7 square miles of land, about 2,511 people per square mile. By 2020 population it ranks 26th of the 133 cities and census-designated towns in Nevada.

What county is Fallon, Nevada in?

Fallon is in Churchill County, in the Central Nevada region. Fallon is the county seat.

What is the ZIP code for Fallon, Nevada?

The ZIP code covering Fallon is 89406. Mailing addresses do not always match town boundaries, so confirm a specific address with USPS.

What area code does Fallon use?

Fallon uses area code 775, which covers all of Nevada outside Clark County.

How far is Fallon from Las Vegas?

Fallon is about 295 miles from Las Vegas in a straight line. Driving distance is longer and depends on the route.

What towns are near Fallon?

The closest is Fallon Station, about 5 miles away. Others nearby include Fernley, Silver Springs, Wadsworth.

What school district serves Fallon?

Public schools in Fallon are run by the Churchill County School District; Nevada has one school district per county. The school a student attends depends on the home address.

What is the cost of living in Fallon, Nevada?

Nevada has no state personal income tax. Sales tax is 6.85% at minimum statewide and higher in some counties (8.375% in Clark County). Housing is the biggest cost difference between towns. See our Nevada moving guide for more on taxes and costs.

How soon do I need a Nevada license and plates after moving to Fallon?

New residents must get a Nevada driver's license and register their vehicles within 30 days of moving to the state.
