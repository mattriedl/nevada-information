About Fernley

Fernley is an incorporated city in Lyon County, in the Carson City & Western Nevada region, with 22,895 residents at the 2020 Census.

Fernley is a city in Lyon County, Nevada, United States, and part of the Reno–Tahoe–Sparks metropolitan area CSA. The city was incorporated in 2001. The population of the city was 22,895 at the 2020 census, making it the 7th most populous city in Nevada.

Fernley was home to the historic and one of the first Amazon.com centers in the world, which has since relocated within the metro area. Naval Air Station (TOPGUN), the U.S. Navy's Naval Strike and Air Warfare Center & TOPGUN training program since 1996, was moved nearby, to Fallon, from Naval Air Station Miramar. The city is home to the Reno-Fernley Raceway. The world's first Tesla Gigafactory, that produces battery packs, energy storage and electric vehicle components is nearby 15 miles west at the Tahoe Reno Industrial Center, and also there as of 2024 an under-construction lithium processing plant.

Fernley, Nevada: common questions

What is the population of Fernley, Nevada?

Fernley had 22,895 residents at the 2020 U.S. Census (up from 19,368 in 2010). The Census Bureau estimated 25,508 residents in mid-2024.

How big is Fernley, Nevada?

Fernley covers about 121.3 square miles of land, about 189 people per square mile. By 2020 population it ranks 15th of the 133 cities and census-designated towns in Nevada.

What county is Fernley, Nevada in?

Fernley is in Lyon County, in the Carson City & Western Nevada region. The county seat is Yerington.

What is the ZIP code for Fernley, Nevada?

ZIP codes that cover part of Fernley include 89408, 89429. Mailing addresses do not always match town boundaries, so confirm a specific address with USPS.

What area code does Fernley use?

Fernley uses area code 775, which covers all of Nevada outside Clark County.

How far is Fernley from Las Vegas?

Fernley is about 314 miles from Las Vegas in a straight line. Driving distance is longer and depends on the route.

What towns are near Fernley?

The closest is Wadsworth, about 7 miles away. Others nearby include Silver Springs, Stagecoach, Nixon.

What school district serves Fernley?

Public schools in Fernley are run by the Lyon County School District; Nevada has one school district per county. The school a student attends depends on the home address.

What is the cost of living in Fernley, Nevada?

Nevada has no state personal income tax. Sales tax is 6.85% at minimum statewide and higher in some counties (8.375% in Clark County). Housing is the biggest cost difference between towns. See our Nevada moving guide for more on taxes and costs.

How soon do I need a Nevada license and plates after moving to Fernley?

New residents must get a Nevada driver's license and register their vehicles within 30 days of moving to the state.
