About Humboldt River Ranch

Humboldt River Ranch is an unincorporated town (census-designated place) in Pershing County, in the Northeastern Nevada region, with 249 residents at the 2020 Census. Because it is not incorporated, Pershing County runs local services such as roads, policing and planning.

Humboldt River Ranch is a census-designated place (CDP) in Pershing County, Nevada, United States. As of the 2020 census, Humboldt River Ranch had a population of 249.

Humboldt River Ranch, Nevada: common questions

What is the population of Humboldt River Ranch, Nevada?

Humboldt River Ranch had 249 residents at the 2020 U.S. Census.

How big is Humboldt River Ranch, Nevada?

Humboldt River Ranch covers about 20.2 square miles of land, about 12 people per square mile. By 2020 population it ranks 94th of the 133 cities and census-designated towns in Nevada.

What county is Humboldt River Ranch, Nevada in?

Humboldt River Ranch is in Pershing County, in the Northeastern Nevada region. It is an unincorporated town, so Pershing County provides local government services. The county seat is Lovelock.

What is the ZIP code for Humboldt River Ranch, Nevada?

The ZIP code covering Humboldt River Ranch is 89419. Mailing addresses do not always match town boundaries, so confirm a specific address with USPS.

What area code does Humboldt River Ranch use?

Humboldt River Ranch uses area code 775, which covers all of Nevada outside Clark County.

How far is Humboldt River Ranch from Las Vegas?

Humboldt River Ranch is about 333 miles from Las Vegas in a straight line. Driving distance is longer and depends on the route.

What towns are near Humboldt River Ranch?

The closest is Unionville, about 8 miles away. Others nearby include Imlay, Lovelock, Grass Valley.

What school district serves Humboldt River Ranch?

Public schools in Humboldt River Ranch are run by the Pershing County School District; Nevada has one school district per county. The school a student attends depends on the home address.

What is the cost of living in Humboldt River Ranch, Nevada?

Nevada has no state personal income tax. Sales tax is 6.85% at minimum statewide and higher in some counties (8.375% in Clark County). Housing is the biggest cost difference between towns. See our Nevada moving guide for more on taxes and costs.

How soon do I need a Nevada license and plates after moving to Humboldt River Ranch?

New residents must get a Nevada driver's license and register their vehicles within 30 days of moving to the state.
