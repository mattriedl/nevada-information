Nevada cities and towns

133 places, from Las Vegas to Jarbidge

Nevada has only 19 incorporated cities, counting Carson City. Many of its biggest communities, including Paradise, Spring Valley, Enterprise and Sunrise Manor around the Las Vegas Strip, are unincorporated towns that the Census Bureau counts as census-designated places (CDPs). This directory covers all 133 of them.
