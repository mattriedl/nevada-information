About Kingsbury

Kingsbury is an unincorporated town (census-designated place) in Douglas County, in the Carson City & Western Nevada region, with 2,313 residents at the 2020 Census. Because it is not incorporated, Douglas County runs local services such as roads, policing and planning.

Kingsbury is a census-designated place (CDP) in Douglas County, Nevada, United States. As of the 2020 census, Kingsbury had a population of 2,313.

Kingsbury, Nevada: common questions

What is the population of Kingsbury, Nevada?

Kingsbury had 2,313 residents at the 2020 U.S. Census (up from 2,152 in 2010).

How big is Kingsbury, Nevada?

Kingsbury covers about 21.8 square miles of land, about 106 people per square mile. By 2020 population it ranks 43rd of the 133 cities and census-designated towns in Nevada.

What county is Kingsbury, Nevada in?

Kingsbury is in Douglas County, in the Carson City & Western Nevada region. It is an unincorporated town, so Douglas County provides local government services. The county seat is Minden.

What is the ZIP code for Kingsbury, Nevada?

ZIP codes that cover part of Kingsbury include 89411, 89449. Mailing addresses do not always match town boundaries, so confirm a specific address with USPS.

What area code does Kingsbury use?

Kingsbury uses area code 775, which covers all of Nevada outside Clark County.

How far is Kingsbury from Las Vegas?

Kingsbury is about 317 miles from Las Vegas in a straight line. Driving distance is longer and depends on the route.

What towns are near Kingsbury?

The closest is Round Hill Village, about 2 miles away. Others nearby include Zephyr Cove, Skyland, Stateline.

What school district serves Kingsbury?

Public schools in Kingsbury are run by the Douglas County School District; Nevada has one school district per county. The school a student attends depends on the home address.

What is the cost of living in Kingsbury, Nevada?

Nevada has no state personal income tax. Sales tax is 6.85% at minimum statewide and higher in some counties (8.375% in Clark County). Housing is the biggest cost difference between towns. See our Nevada moving guide for more on taxes and costs.

How soon do I need a Nevada license and plates after moving to Kingsbury?

New residents must get a Nevada driver's license and register their vehicles within 30 days of moving to the state.
