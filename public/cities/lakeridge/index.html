About Lakeridge

Lakeridge is an unincorporated town (census-designated place) in Douglas County, in the Carson City & Western Nevada region, with 409 residents at the 2020 Census. Because it is not incorporated, Douglas County runs local services such as roads, policing and planning.

Lakeridge, Nevada: common questions

What is the population of Lakeridge, Nevada?

Lakeridge had 409 residents at the 2020 U.S. Census (up from 371 in 2010).

How big is Lakeridge, Nevada?

Lakeridge covers about 1.5 square miles of land, about 281 people per square mile. By 2020 population it ranks 84th of the 133 cities and census-designated towns in Nevada.

What county is Lakeridge, Nevada in?

Lakeridge is in Douglas County, in the Carson City & Western Nevada region. It is an unincorporated town, so Douglas County provides local government services. The county seat is Minden.

What is the ZIP code for Lakeridge, Nevada?

The ZIP code covering Lakeridge is 89413. Mailing addresses do not always match town boundaries, so confirm a specific address with USPS.

What area code does Lakeridge use?

Lakeridge uses area code 775, which covers all of Nevada outside Clark County.

How far is Lakeridge from Las Vegas?

Lakeridge is about 321 miles from Las Vegas in a straight line. Driving distance is longer and depends on the route.

What towns are near Lakeridge?

The closest is Skyland, about 1 miles away. Others nearby include Zephyr Cove, Logan Creek, Round Hill Village.

What school district serves Lakeridge?

Public schools in Lakeridge are run by the Douglas County School District; Nevada has one school district per county. The school a student attends depends on the home address.

What is the cost of living in Lakeridge, Nevada?

Nevada has no state personal income tax. Sales tax is 6.85% at minimum statewide and higher in some counties (8.375% in Clark County). Housing is the biggest cost difference between towns. See our Nevada moving guide for more on taxes and costs.

How soon do I need a Nevada license and plates after moving to Lakeridge?

New residents must get a Nevada driver's license and register their vehicles within 30 days of moving to the state.
