About Mina

Mina is an unincorporated town (census-designated place) in Mineral County, in the Central Nevada region, with 127 residents at the 2020 Census. Because it is not incorporated, Mineral County runs local services such as roads, policing and planning.

Mina is an unincorporated community and census-designated place (CDP) in Mineral County in west-central Nevada, United States. It is located along U.S. Route 95 (38° 23' 25" N 118° 06' 30" W) at an elevation of 4,560 feet (1,390 m). The population at the 2020 census was 127.

Mina, Nevada: common questions

What is the population of Mina, Nevada?

Mina had 127 residents at the 2020 U.S. Census.

How big is Mina, Nevada?

Mina covers about 2.4 square miles of land, about 53 people per square mile. By 2020 population it ranks 112th of the 133 cities and census-designated towns in Nevada.

What county is Mina, Nevada in?

Mina is in Mineral County, in the Central Nevada region. It is an unincorporated town, so Mineral County provides local government services. The county seat is Hawthorne.

What is the ZIP code for Mina, Nevada?

The ZIP code covering Mina is 89422. Mailing addresses do not always match town boundaries, so confirm a specific address with USPS.

What area code does Mina use?

Mina uses area code 775, which covers all of Nevada outside Clark County.

How far is Mina from Las Vegas?

Mina is about 216 miles from Las Vegas in a straight line. Driving distance is longer and depends on the route.

What towns are near Mina?

The closest is Hawthorne, about 29 miles away. Others nearby include Gabbs, Walker Lake, Silver Peak.

What school district serves Mina?

Public schools in Mina are run by the Mineral County School District; Nevada has one school district per county. The school a student attends depends on the home address.

What is the cost of living in Mina, Nevada?

Nevada has no state personal income tax. Sales tax is 6.85% at minimum statewide and higher in some counties (8.375% in Clark County). Housing is the biggest cost difference between towns. See our Nevada moving guide for more on taxes and costs.

How soon do I need a Nevada license and plates after moving to Mina?

New residents must get a Nevada driver's license and register their vehicles within 30 days of moving to the state.
