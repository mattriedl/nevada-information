About Mount Charleston

Mount Charleston is an unincorporated town (census-designated place) in Clark County, in the Las Vegas & Southern Nevada region, with 314 residents at the 2020 Census. Because it is not incorporated, Clark County runs local services such as roads, policing and planning.

Mount Charleston is an unincorporated town and census-designated place in Clark County, Nevada, United States. The population was 357 at the 2010 census.

The town of Mount Charleston is named for nearby Mount Charleston whose Charleston Peak at 11,916 feet (3,632 m) is the highest point in Clark County. The town of Mount Charleston is in a valley of the Spring Mountains to the northwest of Las Vegas, noted for its hiking trails. It is also known for its Retreat on Charleston Peak, a 64-room hotel. At an elevation of approximately 7,500 feet, temperatures are much lower than in Las Vegas, which has an elevation of about 2,000 feet, making it a popular place for Las Vegans to vacation. The mean high temperature is 20.4 degrees (Fahrenheit) cooler than in Las Vegas. The area is also known as a vacation village for wealthy Las Vegas residents.

Mount Charleston, Nevada: common questions

What is the population of Mount Charleston, Nevada?

Mount Charleston had 314 residents at the 2020 U.S. Census.

How big is Mount Charleston, Nevada?

Mount Charleston covers about 29.3 square miles of land, about 11 people per square mile. By 2020 population it ranks 89th of the 133 cities and census-designated towns in Nevada.

What county is Mount Charleston, Nevada in?

Mount Charleston is in Clark County, in the Las Vegas & Southern Nevada region. It is an unincorporated town, so Clark County provides local government services. The county seat is Las Vegas.

What is the ZIP code for Mount Charleston, Nevada?

The ZIP code covering Mount Charleston is 89124. Mailing addresses do not always match town boundaries, so confirm a specific address with USPS.

What area code does Mount Charleston use?

Mount Charleston uses area codes 702 and 725, which overlay each other across Clark County, so local calls need all ten digits.

How far is Mount Charleston from Las Vegas?

Mount Charleston is about 20 miles from Las Vegas in a straight line. Driving distance is longer and depends on the route.

What towns are near Mount Charleston?

The closest is Summerlin South, about 18 miles away. Others nearby include Blue Diamond, Las Vegas, Pahrump.

What school district serves Mount Charleston?

Public schools in Mount Charleston are run by the Clark County School District; Nevada has one school district per county. The school a student attends depends on the home address.

What is the cost of living in Mount Charleston, Nevada?

Nevada has no state personal income tax. Sales tax is 6.85% at minimum statewide and higher in some counties (8.375% in Clark County). Housing is the biggest cost difference between towns. See our Nevada moving guide for more on taxes and costs.

How soon do I need a Nevada license and plates after moving to Mount Charleston?

New residents must get a Nevada driver's license and register their vehicles within 30 days of moving to the state.
