About Oasis

Oasis is an unincorporated town (census-designated place) in Elko County, in the Northeastern Nevada region, with 4 residents at the 2020 Census. Because it is not incorporated, Elko County runs local services such as roads, policing and planning.

Oasis is a census-designated place (CDP) located in eastern Elko County, Nevada, United States, at the junction of State Route 233 and Interstate 80, 31 miles (50 km) northwest of the Utah border and 77 miles (124 km) east of Elko. As of the 2020 census, Oasis had a population of 4.

Oasis, Nevada: common questions

What is the population of Oasis, Nevada?

Oasis had 4 residents at the 2020 U.S. Census.

How big is Oasis, Nevada?

Oasis covers about 0.9 square miles of land, about 4 people per square mile. By 2020 population it ranks 133rd of the 133 cities and census-designated towns in Nevada.

What county is Oasis, Nevada in?

Oasis is in Elko County, in the Northeastern Nevada region. It is an unincorporated town, so Elko County provides local government services. The county seat is Elko.

What is the ZIP code for Oasis, Nevada?

The ZIP code covering Oasis is 89835. Mailing addresses do not always match town boundaries, so confirm a specific address with USPS.

What area code does Oasis use?

Oasis uses area code 775, which covers all of Nevada outside Clark County.

How far is Oasis from Las Vegas?

Oasis is about 334 miles from Las Vegas in a straight line. Driving distance is longer and depends on the route.

What towns are near Oasis?

The closest is Montello, about 22 miles away. Others nearby include Wells, West Wendover, Lamoille.

What school district serves Oasis?

Public schools in Oasis are run by the Elko County School District; Nevada has one school district per county. The school a student attends depends on the home address.

What is the cost of living in Oasis, Nevada?

Nevada has no state personal income tax. Sales tax is 6.85% at minimum statewide and higher in some counties (8.375% in Clark County). Housing is the biggest cost difference between towns. See our Nevada moving guide for more on taxes and costs.

How soon do I need a Nevada license and plates after moving to Oasis?

New residents must get a Nevada driver's license and register their vehicles within 30 days of moving to the state.
