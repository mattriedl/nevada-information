About Osino

Osino is an unincorporated town (census-designated place) in Elko County, in the Northeastern Nevada region, with 668 residents at the 2020 Census. Because it is not incorporated, Elko County runs local services such as roads, policing and planning.

Osino is a census-designated place (CDP) along the Humboldt River in central Elko County, Nevada, United States. As of the 2020 census, Osino had a population of 668.

Osino, Nevada: common questions

What is the population of Osino, Nevada?

Osino had 668 residents at the 2020 U.S. Census.

How big is Osino, Nevada?

Osino covers about 2.6 square miles of land, about 259 people per square mile. By 2020 population it ranks 76th of the 133 cities and census-designated towns in Nevada.

What county is Osino, Nevada in?

Osino is in Elko County, in the Northeastern Nevada region. It is an unincorporated town, so Elko County provides local government services. The county seat is Elko.

What is the ZIP code for Osino, Nevada?

The ZIP code covering Osino is 89801. Mailing addresses do not always match town boundaries, so confirm a specific address with USPS.

What area code does Osino use?

Osino uses area code 775, which covers all of Nevada outside Clark County.

How far is Osino from Las Vegas?

Osino is about 326 miles from Las Vegas in a straight line. Driving distance is longer and depends on the route.

What towns are near Osino?

The closest is Elko, about 9 miles away. Others nearby include Spring Creek, Lamoille, Carlin.

What school district serves Osino?

Public schools in Osino are run by the Elko County School District; Nevada has one school district per county. The school a student attends depends on the home address.

What is the cost of living in Osino, Nevada?

Nevada has no state personal income tax. Sales tax is 6.85% at minimum statewide and higher in some counties (8.375% in Clark County). Housing is the biggest cost difference between towns. See our Nevada moving guide for more on taxes and costs.

How soon do I need a Nevada license and plates after moving to Osino?

New residents must get a Nevada driver's license and register their vehicles within 30 days of moving to the state.
