About Smith Valley

Smith Valley is an unincorporated town (census-designated place) in Lyon County, in the Carson City & Western Nevada region, with 1,710 residents at the 2020 Census. Because it is not incorporated, Lyon County runs local services such as roads, policing and planning.

Smith Valley is a census-designated place (CDP) in Lyon County, Nevada, United States. As of the 2020 census, Smith Valley had a population of 1,710.

Smith Valley, Nevada: common questions

What is the population of Smith Valley, Nevada?

Smith Valley had 1,710 residents at the 2020 U.S. Census (up from 1,603 in 2010).

How big is Smith Valley, Nevada?

Smith Valley covers about 102.0 square miles of land, about 17 people per square mile. By 2020 population it ranks 48th of the 133 cities and census-designated towns in Nevada.

What county is Smith Valley, Nevada in?

Smith Valley is in Lyon County, in the Carson City & Western Nevada region. It is an unincorporated town, so Lyon County provides local government services. The county seat is Yerington.

What is the ZIP code for Smith Valley, Nevada?

ZIP codes that cover part of Smith Valley include 89430, 89444. Mailing addresses do not always match town boundaries, so confirm a specific address with USPS.

What area code does Smith Valley use?

Smith Valley uses area code 775, which covers all of Nevada outside Clark County.

How far is Smith Valley from Las Vegas?

Smith Valley is about 286 miles from Las Vegas in a straight line. Driving distance is longer and depends on the route.

What towns are near Smith Valley?

The closest is Topaz Ranch Estates, about 8 miles away. Others nearby include Topaz Lake, Double Spring, Carter Springs.

What school district serves Smith Valley?

Public schools in Smith Valley are run by the Lyon County School District; Nevada has one school district per county. The school a student attends depends on the home address.

What is the cost of living in Smith Valley, Nevada?

Nevada has no state personal income tax. Sales tax is 6.85% at minimum statewide and higher in some counties (8.375% in Clark County). Housing is the biggest cost difference between towns. See our Nevada moving guide for more on taxes and costs.

How soon do I need a Nevada license and plates after moving to Smith Valley?

New residents must get a Nevada driver's license and register their vehicles within 30 days of moving to the state.
