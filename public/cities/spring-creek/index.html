About Spring Creek

Spring Creek is an unincorporated town (census-designated place) in Elko County, in the Northeastern Nevada region, with 14,967 residents at the 2020 Census. Because it is not incorporated, Elko County runs local services such as roads, policing and planning.

Spring Creek is a census-designated place (CDP) in central Elko County, in northeastern Nevada in the western United States. It mainly serves as a bedroom community for the businesses and industries in and around the nearby city of Elko. It is part of the Elko Micropolitan Statistical Area. As of the 2020 census, Spring Creek had a population of 14,967.

Spring Creek, Nevada: common questions

What is the population of Spring Creek, Nevada?

Spring Creek had 14,967 residents at the 2020 U.S. Census.

How big is Spring Creek, Nevada?

Spring Creek covers about 53.7 square miles of land, about 279 people per square mile. By 2020 population it ranks 21st of the 133 cities and census-designated towns in Nevada.

What county is Spring Creek, Nevada in?

Spring Creek is in Elko County, in the Northeastern Nevada region. It is an unincorporated town, so Elko County provides local government services. The county seat is Elko.

What is the ZIP code for Spring Creek, Nevada?

The ZIP code covering Spring Creek is 89815. Mailing addresses do not always match town boundaries, so confirm a specific address with USPS.

What area code does Spring Creek use?

Spring Creek uses area code 775, which covers all of Nevada outside Clark County.

How far is Spring Creek from Las Vegas?

Spring Creek is about 312 miles from Las Vegas in a straight line. Driving distance is longer and depends on the route.

What towns are near Spring Creek?

The closest is Lamoille, about 6 miles away. Others nearby include Elko, Osino, Carlin.

What school district serves Spring Creek?

Public schools in Spring Creek are run by the Elko County School District; Nevada has one school district per county. The school a student attends depends on the home address.

What is the cost of living in Spring Creek, Nevada?

Nevada has no state personal income tax. Sales tax is 6.85% at minimum statewide and higher in some counties (8.375% in Clark County). Housing is the biggest cost difference between towns. See our Nevada moving guide for more on taxes and costs.

How soon do I need a Nevada license and plates after moving to Spring Creek?

New residents must get a Nevada driver's license and register their vehicles within 30 days of moving to the state.
