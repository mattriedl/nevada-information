About Summerlin South

Summerlin South is an unincorporated town (census-designated place) in Clark County, in the Las Vegas & Southern Nevada region, with 30,744 residents at the 2020 Census. Because it is not incorporated, Clark County runs local services such as roads, policing and planning.

Summerlin South, also seen on maps as South Summerlin, is a census-designated place (CDP) in Clark County, Nevada, United States, on the western edge of the Las Vegas Valley and adjacent to the Red Rock Canyon National Conservation Area. It is named because it is a southward extension of the master-planned community of Summerlin. Nearly all of Summerlin South is in ZIP code 89135. The population was 30,744 at the 2020 Census.

The Summerlin South Community Association is the main management entity of the area – it is a homeowners association for the entire community.

Summerlin South, Nevada: common questions

What is the population of Summerlin South, Nevada?

Summerlin South had 30,744 residents at the 2020 U.S. Census.

How big is Summerlin South, Nevada?

Summerlin South covers about 9.7 square miles of land, about 3,174 people per square mile. By 2020 population it ranks 14th of the 133 cities and census-designated towns in Nevada.

What county is Summerlin South, Nevada in?

Summerlin South is in Clark County, in the Las Vegas & Southern Nevada region. It is an unincorporated town, so Clark County provides local government services. The county seat is Las Vegas.

What is the ZIP code for Summerlin South, Nevada?

The ZIP code covering Summerlin South is 89135. Mailing addresses do not always match town boundaries, so confirm a specific address with USPS.

What area code does Summerlin South use?

Summerlin South uses area codes 702 and 725, which overlay each other across Clark County, so local calls need all ten digits.

How far is Summerlin South from Las Vegas?

Summerlin South is about 8 miles from Las Vegas in a straight line. Driving distance is longer and depends on the route.

What towns are near Summerlin South?

The closest is Spring Valley, about 4 miles away. Others nearby include Blue Diamond, Las Vegas, Enterprise.

What school district serves Summerlin South?

Public schools in Summerlin South are run by the Clark County School District; Nevada has one school district per county. The school a student attends depends on the home address.

What is the cost of living in Summerlin South, Nevada?

Nevada has no state personal income tax. Sales tax is 6.85% at minimum statewide and higher in some counties (8.375% in Clark County). Housing is the biggest cost difference between towns. See our Nevada moving guide for more on taxes and costs.

How soon do I need a Nevada license and plates after moving to Summerlin South?

New residents must get a Nevada driver's license and register their vehicles within 30 days of moving to the state.
