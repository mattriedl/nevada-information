About Sunrise Manor

Sunrise Manor is an unincorporated town (census-designated place) in Clark County, in the Las Vegas & Southern Nevada region, with 205,618 residents at the 2020 Census. Because it is not incorporated, Clark County runs local services such as roads, policing and planning.

Sunrise Manor, Nevada: common questions

What is the population of Sunrise Manor, Nevada?

Sunrise Manor had 205,618 residents at the 2020 U.S. Census (up from 189,372 in 2010).

How big is Sunrise Manor, Nevada?

Sunrise Manor covers about 33.7 square miles of land, about 6,099 people per square mile. By 2020 population it ranks 7th of the 133 cities and census-designated towns in Nevada.

What county is Sunrise Manor, Nevada in?

Sunrise Manor is in Clark County, in the Las Vegas & Southern Nevada region. It is an unincorporated town, so Clark County provides local government services. The county seat is Las Vegas.

What is the ZIP code for Sunrise Manor, Nevada?

ZIP codes that cover part of Sunrise Manor include 89104, 89110, 89115, 89121, 89122, 89142, 89156. Mailing addresses do not always match town boundaries, so confirm a specific address with USPS.

What area code does Sunrise Manor use?

Sunrise Manor uses area codes 702 and 725, which overlay each other across Clark County, so local calls need all ten digits.

How far is Sunrise Manor from Las Vegas?

Sunrise Manor is about 13 miles from Las Vegas in a straight line. Driving distance is longer and depends on the route.

What towns are near Sunrise Manor?

The closest is Nellis AFB, about 5 miles away. Others nearby include Whitney, Winchester, North Las Vegas.

What school district serves Sunrise Manor?

Public schools in Sunrise Manor are run by the Clark County School District; Nevada has one school district per county. The school a student attends depends on the home address.

What is the cost of living in Sunrise Manor, Nevada?

Nevada has no state personal income tax. Sales tax is 6.85% at minimum statewide and higher in some counties (8.375% in Clark County). Housing is the biggest cost difference between towns. See our Nevada moving guide for more on taxes and costs.

How soon do I need a Nevada license and plates after moving to Sunrise Manor?

New residents must get a Nevada driver's license and register their vehicles within 30 days of moving to the state.
