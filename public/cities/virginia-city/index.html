About Virginia City

Virginia City is an unincorporated town (census-designated place) in Storey County, in the Reno-Tahoe region, with 787 residents at the 2020 Census. Because it is not incorporated, Storey County runs local services such as roads, policing and planning.

Virginia City is a historic town in Storey County, Nevada, United States. It was established in 1859 after the discovery of the Comstock Lode, one of the first major silver deposits in the United States. It rapidly developed into one of the most important mining centers of the American West. At its peak in the mid-1870s, Virginia City had an estimated population of 25,000 residents and served as a center of mining, finance, engineering, and innovation.

Mining output declined after 1878, and the population fell sharply, but many historic buildings survived. The town played a significant role in Nevada’s economic development. It is closely associated with writers such as Mark Twain, who began his career there. In the 20th century, Virginia City’s historic area was preserved, and the town was designated a National Historic Landmark District in 1961. As of the 2020 census, the population was 787.

Virginia City, Nevada: common questions

What is the population of Virginia City, Nevada?

Virginia City had 787 residents at the 2020 U.S. Census.

How big is Virginia City, Nevada?

Virginia City covers about 0.9 square miles of land, about 908 people per square mile. By 2020 population it ranks 72nd of the 133 cities and census-designated towns in Nevada.

What county is Virginia City, Nevada in?

Virginia City is in Storey County, in the Reno-Tahoe region. It is an unincorporated town, so Storey County provides local government services. Virginia City is the county seat.

What is the ZIP code for Virginia City, Nevada?

The ZIP code covering Virginia City is 89440. Mailing addresses do not always match town boundaries, so confirm a specific address with USPS.

What area code does Virginia City use?

Virginia City uses area code 775, which covers all of Nevada outside Clark County.

How far is Virginia City from Las Vegas?

Virginia City is about 320 miles from Las Vegas in a straight line. Driving distance is longer and depends on the route.

What towns are near Virginia City?

The closest is Silver City, about 3 miles away. Others nearby include Dayton, Washoe Valley, Carson City.

What school district serves Virginia City?

Public schools in Virginia City are run by the Storey County School District; Nevada has one school district per county. The school a student attends depends on the home address.

What is the cost of living in Virginia City, Nevada?

Nevada has no state personal income tax. Sales tax is 6.85% at minimum statewide and higher in some counties (8.375% in Clark County). Housing is the biggest cost difference between towns. See our Nevada moving guide for more on taxes and costs.

How soon do I need a Nevada license and plates after moving to Virginia City?

New residents must get a Nevada driver's license and register their vehicles within 30 days of moving to the state.
