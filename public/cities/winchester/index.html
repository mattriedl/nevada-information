About Winchester

Winchester is an unincorporated town (census-designated place) in Clark County, in the Las Vegas & Southern Nevada region, with 36,403 residents at the 2020 Census. Because it is not incorporated, Clark County runs local services such as roads, policing and planning.

Winchester is an unincorporated town and census-designated place (CDP) in Clark County, Nevada, United States that contains part of the Las Vegas Strip. It is one of a number of CDPs in the unincorporated urbanized area directly south of Las Vegas. The population was 36,403 at the 2020 census. It is governed by the Clark County Commission with advice from the Winchester Town Advisory Board. "Winchester, NV" does not appear in postal addresses; the United States Postal Service has assigned "Las Vegas, NV" as the place name for the ZIP codes containing Winchester.

Winchester, Nevada: common questions

What is the population of Winchester, Nevada?

Winchester had 36,403 residents at the 2020 U.S. Census (up from 27,978 in 2010).

How big is Winchester, Nevada?

Winchester covers about 5.0 square miles of land, about 7,342 people per square mile. By 2020 population it ranks 13th of the 133 cities and census-designated towns in Nevada.

What county is Winchester, Nevada in?

Winchester is in Clark County, in the Las Vegas & Southern Nevada region. It is an unincorporated town, so Clark County provides local government services. The county seat is Las Vegas.

What is the ZIP code for Winchester, Nevada?

ZIP codes that cover part of Winchester include 89102, 89109, 89121, 89169. Mailing addresses do not always match town boundaries, so confirm a specific address with USPS.

What area code does Winchester use?

Winchester uses area codes 702 and 725, which overlay each other across Clark County, so local calls need all ten digits.

How far is Winchester from Las Vegas?

Winchester is about 10 miles from Las Vegas in a straight line. Driving distance is longer and depends on the route.

What towns are near Winchester?

The closest is Paradise, about 3 miles away. Others nearby include Sunrise Manor, Whitney, Spring Valley.

What school district serves Winchester?

Public schools in Winchester are run by the Clark County School District; Nevada has one school district per county. The school a student attends depends on the home address.

What is the cost of living in Winchester, Nevada?

Nevada has no state personal income tax. Sales tax is 6.85% at minimum statewide and higher in some counties (8.375% in Clark County). Housing is the biggest cost difference between towns. See our Nevada moving guide for more on taxes and costs.

How soon do I need a Nevada license and plates after moving to Winchester?

New residents must get a Nevada driver's license and register their vehicles within 30 days of moving to the state.
