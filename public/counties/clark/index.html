Clark County: common questions

What is the population of Clark County, Nevada?

Clark County had 2,265,461 residents at the 2020 Census, the 1st most of Nevada's 17 counties. The 2024 estimate is 2,398,871.

What is the county seat of Clark County?

The county seat is Las Vegas.

How big is Clark County?

Clark County covers about 7,891 square miles of land, the 6th largest of Nevada's counties.

What cities and towns are in Clark County?

Clark County includes Las Vegas, Henderson, North Las Vegas, Enterprise, Spring Valley, Sunrise Manor, Paradise, Whitney and 17 other communities.

What school district serves Clark County?

The Clark County School District runs public schools across the whole county.

How many people in Clark County live in a city or town?

About 99% of residents live inside a city or census-designated town; the rest live in rural areas outside them.
