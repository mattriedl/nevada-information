Nevada's counties

16 counties and one independent city

Nevada is divided into 16 counties plus Carson City, an independent city that serves as its own county. Clark County alone holds 73% of the state's people; Esmeralda County, the least populous, had 729 residents at the 2020 Census.

Each county also runs its own school district, so the county you live in decides which district your children attend.
