Lander County: common questions

What is the population of Lander County, Nevada?

Lander County had 5,734 residents at the 2020 Census, the 12th most of Nevada's 17 counties. The 2024 estimate is 5,785.

What is the county seat of Lander County?

The county seat is Battle Mountain.

How big is Lander County?

Lander County covers about 5,519 square miles of land, the 9th largest of Nevada's counties.

What cities and towns are in Lander County?

Lander County includes Battle Mountain, Kingston, Austin.

What school district serves Lander County?

The Lander County School District runs public schools across the whole county.

How many people in Lander County live in a city or town?

About 71% of residents live inside a city or census-designated town; the rest live in rural areas outside them.
