About Lyon County

Lyon County is in Nevada's Carson City & Western Nevada region.

Lyon County is a county in the U.S. state of Nevada. As of the 2020 census, its population was 59,235. Its county seat is Yerington. Lyon County is a part of the Reno metropolitan area.

Lyon County: common questions

What is the population of Lyon County, Nevada?

Lyon County had 59,235 residents at the 2020 Census, the 3rd most of Nevada's 17 counties. The 2024 estimate is 63,718.

What is the county seat of Lyon County?

The county seat is Yerington.

How big is Lyon County?

Lyon County covers about 2,003 square miles of land, the 14th largest of Nevada's counties.

What cities and towns are in Lyon County?

Lyon County includes Fernley, Dayton, Silver Springs, Yerington, Stagecoach, Smith Valley, Silver City.

What school district serves Lyon County?

The Lyon County School District runs public schools across the whole county.

How many people in Lyon County live in a city or town?

About 86% of residents live inside a city or census-designated town; the rest live in rural areas outside them.
