About Storey County

Storey County is in Nevada's Reno-Tahoe region.

Storey County is a county located in the U.S. state of Nevada. As of the 2020 census, the population was 4,104, making it the third-least populous county, but one of the fastest-growing economies in Nevada. In 2018, over 18,000 people were employed in the county. Its area is 264 square miles (680 square kilometers), making it the smallest county in Nevada in terms of area (though the independent city of Carson City is smaller). Its county seat is Virginia City. Storey County is part of the Reno-Sparks metropolitan area. The population of the county has fluctuated significantly throughout the years, from a high of 16,115 in 1880 to a low of 568 in 1960.

Storey County: common questions

What is the population of Storey County, Nevada?

Storey County had 4,104 residents at the 2020 Census, the 15th most of Nevada's 17 counties. The 2024 estimate is 4,112.

What is the county seat of Storey County?

The county seat is Virginia City.

How big is Storey County?

Storey County covers about 263 square miles of land, the 16th largest of Nevada's counties.

What cities and towns are in Storey County?

Storey County includes Virginia City.

What school district serves Storey County?

The Storey County School District runs public schools across the whole county.

How many people in Storey County live in a city or town?

About 19% of residents live inside a city or census-designated town; the rest live in rural areas outside them.
