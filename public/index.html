Population, ZIP codes, schools, weather and moving help for all 133 cities and towns in the Silver State.

    Nevada had 3,104,614 residents at the 2020 Census and an estimated 3,267,467 by 2024. Only 19 of its communities are incorporated cities; most people outside Las Vegas, Henderson, North Las Vegas, Reno and Sparks live in unincorporated towns run by their county. This site covers both.

    Six regions

    • Las Vegas & Southern NevadaClark County

      Clark County holds about three of every four Nevadans: the Las Vegas Valley cities of Las Vegas, Henderson and North Las Vegas, the large unincorporated towns of Paradise, Spring Valley, Enterprise and Sunrise Manor that surround the Strip, and outlying communities such as Boulder City, Mesquite, Laughlin and the Moapa Valley.

    • Reno-TahoeWashoe County, Storey County

      Reno and Sparks anchor the Truckee Meadows at the foot of the Sierra Nevada, with the Comstock mining town of Virginia City in the hills above and the Nevada shore of Lake Tahoe at Incline Village.

    • Carson City & Western NevadaCarson City, Douglas County, Lyon County

      The state capital, Carson City, sits between the Carson Range and the Pine Nut Mountains.

    • Northeastern NevadaElko County, Humboldt County, Pershing County, Lander County, Eureka County

      Interstate 80 follows the Humboldt River across this high, open country of cattle ranches and gold mines.

    • Central NevadaChurchill County, Mineral County, Nye County, Esmeralda County

      From the farm fields of Fallon to the old silver camps of Tonopah and Goldfield, central Nevada is a long stretch of basins and ranges crossed by US 50 and US 95.

    • Eastern Nevada & Great BasinWhite Pine County, Lincoln County

      Ely, on US 50 and US 93, is the hub of White Pine County and the gateway to Great Basin National Park, home to Wheeler Peak and ancient bristlecone pines.
