Privacy policy

How nevadainformation.com handles information.

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Information we collect

This site has no accounts or sign-up forms. If you email us, we keep your message and address only to reply.

Weather and fonts

City pages request a forecast from Open-Meteo using the town's coordinates, not your location. Fonts load from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address as part of the request.

Analytics

We may use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are useful. It sets cookies and collects data such as your browser, approximate location and the pages you view. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on or by blocking cookies.

Advertising

If we show ads, advertisers or ad networks may use cookies to measure them. We do not sell personal information.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact@nevadainformation.com.
