Carson City & Western Nevada

167,362 residents, 29 cities and towns

The state capital, Carson City, sits between the Carson Range and the Pine Nut Mountains. South of it, the Carson Valley towns of Minden and Gardnerville spread across ranch land, and Douglas County also takes in the Lake Tahoe casinos at Stateline. Lyon County to the east covers Dayton, Fernley, Yerington and Silver Springs, one of the faster-growing parts of the state.

Living here

Government and health-care jobs in Carson City, commuting distance to Reno, and more land per dollar than the Truckee Meadows.

Visiting

The Nevada State Capitol and museums, Genoa (Nevada's first settlement), the Carson Valley and Lake Tahoe at Stateline.
