Las Vegas & Southern Nevada

2,265,461 residents, 25 cities and towns

Clark County holds about three of every four Nevadans: the Las Vegas Valley cities of Las Vegas, Henderson and North Las Vegas, the large unincorporated towns of Paradise, Spring Valley, Enterprise and Sunrise Manor that surround the Strip, and outlying communities such as Boulder City, Mesquite, Laughlin and the Moapa Valley. It is Mojave Desert country, with hot summers, mild winters, Lake Mead and Red Rock Canyon close to town.

Living here

The state's biggest job market (hospitality, gaming, construction, health care and logistics), the widest choice of housing, and RTC buses across the valley.

Visiting

The Strip and downtown Fremont Street, Red Rock Canyon, Valley of Fire State Park, Hoover Dam and Lake Mead.
