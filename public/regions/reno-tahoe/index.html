Reno-Tahoe

490,596 residents, 18 cities and towns

Reno and Sparks anchor the Truckee Meadows at the foot of the Sierra Nevada, with the Comstock mining town of Virginia City in the hills above and the Nevada shore of Lake Tahoe at Incline Village. Washoe County runs north past Pyramid Lake to the Black Rock Desert. Four distinct seasons, snow in the mountains and a fast-growing industrial base along Interstate 80 east of Sparks.

Living here

A second big job market (logistics, manufacturing, the University of Nevada, Reno and tourism), and a quick drive to Lake Tahoe ski areas.

Visiting

Downtown Reno and the Truckee River, Lake Tahoe's east shore, Pyramid Lake and Virginia City's boardwalks.
